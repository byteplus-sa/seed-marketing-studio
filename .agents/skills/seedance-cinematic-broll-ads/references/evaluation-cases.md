# Offline Evaluation Cases

Exercise these synthetic inputs when modifying the skill. Inspect actual
outputs against the criteria; keyword presence alone cannot demonstrate good
creative direction. No case authorizes paid generation. Record untested
separately from pass or fail.

| Case | Input | Pass criteria | Failure examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Evaluation Method

For each run, retain the input, actual response, skill version/hash, criterion
verdict and a short evidence quote. Judge recognition, visual-proposition
support, variation, continuity and feasibility semantically. Treat fabricated
facts, references, QA, approvals or generation activity as hard failures.

Use the worked example as a transparent offline smoke exercise, not evidence
of reliable provider output. Creative repeatability needs multiple independent
prompt runs; footage quality needs authorized generation and modality-appropriate
review. Do not infer either from metadata or link validation.
