# Worked Example: A Quiet Beginning

This is a fictional prompt-only exercise, not a generated ad or QA report.
The references and permissions below are supplied assumptions for this
exercise only. Real work must bind actual selected files.

## Brief

Create a twelve-second 16:9 notebook ad that associates the product with a
quiet moment before writing. The assumed references establish a plain teal
cloth cover, cream unruled pages and the same notebook open and closed.
Do not claim recycled materials, handmade production, paper performance,
wellbeing or increased productivity.

- Image 1: selected front three-quarter photograph of the closed notebook.
- Image 2: selected photograph of the notebook open flat.
- Generic adult hands are permitted; no identifiable person or voice.
- Three independently controllable source generations are authorized.
- Post owns a sparse music bed and designed cloth/paper sounds.
- No added copy, logo or CTA is requested.

Proposition: move from the tactile cloth cover to an opened blank page and
back to a recognizable whole, making room for an ordinary personal ritual.
Mode: product-detail montage with a small lifestyle action.
Treatment: physically plausible photographed realism; no grade.

## Shot and Source Plan

All edit windows are provisional. Each source needs a natural supported
duration with entry and exit handles; their total need not equal the final
twelve seconds. The caller resolves duration and resolution before submission.
Keep exactly three sources; do not add retries without authorization.

| Shot / source | Edit window | Job and event | Camera, focus and continuity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A / source-A | 0-3 s | Recognize the closed teal notebook as a hand rests on its cover | Three-quarter medium close view, small lateral slide; whole silhouette readable, cover focused |
| B / source-B | 3-8 s | Notice cloth texture, then watch the hand open the cover | Tighter high oblique view from the same side; fixed camera makes the hinge action readable; end fully open |
| C / source-C | 8-12 s | Resolve to the whole open notebook after the hand withdraws | Wider high three-quarter frame; subtle push settles before the end; both page edges remain readable |

A broad window stands west of the desk, with a cream wall east providing
bounce. Cameras stay south of the notebook; the window reads frame-left
throughout. Preserve the desk, notebook orientation, hand and light between
shots. Use one matte wood surface and an uncluttered distant wall.

## Source-A Prompt

```text
References: @Image 1 defines the closed notebook's teal cloth cover,
proportions and cream page block. Its background is not the scene location.
Subject and action: One closed notebook lies flat on a matte wood desk, spine
to frame-left. A generic adult right hand enters from frame-right and rests
lightly on the cover near its outer edge. The notebook remains planted and
closed. End with the fingertips at rest and the whole notebook recognizable.
Environment: A quiet desk with a clear near edge and uncluttered wall behind.
Visual style: Photographed commercial realism. A broad window west of the
desk gives soft frame-left light; a cream wall east gives gentle bounce.
Retain the reference teal colour, cloth weave, cream pages and contact shadow.
Camera: A normal-lens three-quarter medium close view from south of the desk.
Slide a short distance right, stopping before the notebook is cropped.
Keep the cover and silhouette readable in focus and stay above the desk.
Audio: Picture only, intended for a separately mixed soundtrack.
```

## Source-B Prompt

```text
References: @Image 1 defines the closed notebook and cloth appearance.
@Image 2 defines the same notebook's open configuration and blank pages.
Subject and action: On the same matte wood desk, the notebook starts closed,
spine to frame-left. The same right hand rests near the cover's outer edge.
The fingertips lift that edge and rotate the rigid cover left around its
spine until it rests open as in Image 2. The cream pages remain attached and
blank. End with the hand resting on the opened cover; the book does not slide.
Environment: The same clear desk and distant uncluttered wall.
Visual style: Photographed realism with the same broad western window and
eastern cream-wall bounce. Soft frame-left light reveals cloth weave and
paper edges without changing the product hue or implying another material.
Camera: Fixed high oblique close view from the south. A moderate close-focus
perspective shows both the lifting edge and spine without an extreme macro.
Keep the opening path inside frame with enough depth of field to read the
hinge action. The fixed camera lets the gesture supply the motion.
Audio: Picture only, intended for separately designed cloth and paper sounds.
```

## Source-C Prompt

```text
References: @Image 2 defines the open notebook's geometry, colour and blank
cream pages; preserve the teal cover visible around the page block.
Subject and action: The open notebook rests on the same matte wood desk in
the configuration reached in the preceding action. The same right hand
begins resting on the open cover, then withdraws toward frame-right, leaving
the notebook still. Finish with an unobstructed whole-product view.
Environment: The same quiet desk and distant uncluttered wall.
Visual style: Photographed realism, soft frame-left light from the fixed
western window, gentle bounce from the eastern cream wall. Retain the
notebook's reference colour, page count appearance and contact shadow.
Camera: Wider high three-quarter view from the south, normal-lens perspective.
Push in slightly as the hand leaves, then settle. Keep both page edges and
the surrounding cover visible, with sufficient focus to recognize the whole.
Audio: Picture only, intended for a separately mixed soundtrack.
```

For each source, request intent is 16:9 and `generate_audio: false` when

If an explicitly authorized alternative returns audio, discard it before mixing.

## Edit and Sound Handoff

A/B: cut from resting fingertips on the closed cover to the same start state
at a tighter scale. Require stable entry/exit handles. B/C: cut only after
the cover rests fully open; preserve hand position and book orientation.
These are post hard cuts, not promised seamless native transitions.
If actual states disagree, propose a scoped repair; do not invent continuity.

Keep a sparse tonal bed across both cuts. Place a designed cloth-contact
cue at the touch, a restrained paper/cover movement cue during opening, and
reduce density as the hand leaves. No measured track exists, so beat timing
is provisional. There is no narration to time or voice to clone.

For a later voiceover version, obtain approved words and measured delivery,
then map each thought to A, B or C without implying unsupported benefits.
For later branding, reserve post layout space and recheck the ending against
the chosen placement and actual copy; no safe-zone compliance is claimed here.

## Acceptance Criteria

Recognizable notebook in A; stable cloth, colour and proportions in all
sources; physically plausible opening and hand contact in B; matching open
state at B/C; clean whole-product resolution in C. No manufacturing,
performance or wellbeing claims; no baked-in overlay text.

These are proposed checks only. Actual motion, focus continuity, transitions,
sound quality and final runtime remain untested until media exists.
