# Worked Example: A Bottle in the Skyline

Hypothetical prompt-only exercise. No rig, calibrated geometry, generation or
media inspection exists. Reference selections below are assumed for this example
and must be grounded in actual files before production.

## Creative contrast before solving geometry

The skyline study below is useful only if its opening actually creates a
misreading; miniature blocks beside a recognizable bottle are not enough.
For an open fictional rectangular-bottle brief, propose an apparent doorway:
the bottle's dark cap aligns with a lintel on a background flat, and two nearer
side flats conceal its outer contours. A controlled separation of the flats
reveals the bottle already standing behind them, unchanged in scale and support.
This is proposed generated staging, not a validated rig or real product size.

Quiet version: lock the camera, let the opening read, then move the two flats
aside and hold the exposed product. Expressive version: a wider native shot
after the reveal shows the separated planes, reinterpreting the first view.
Use that cut only when permitted; retain one source under a one-generation cap.
If the brief says conceal the mechanism, omit the explanatory wide.

**Weak/repair test:** Before refining centimeters or adding an orbit, ask what
the viewer could plausibly mistake the opening for and which visible evidence
changes that reading. A failed misreading is a creative defect even if every
object remains geometrically stable.

## Brief and Authority

A ten-second 16:9 ad for a plain amber rectangular bottle with a square cap.
Exactly one continuous source, no cuts or retries. A forced-perspective opening
makes the bottle appear part of a miniature skyline. A small lateral camera
move reveals that it stands closer to the viewer on the same tabletop.
The user wants the mechanism disclosed, not concealed.

- Image 1: selected acquired front photograph of the closed bottle.
- Image 2: selected front three-quarter photograph of the same closed bottle.
- Canonical descriptor: `amber rectangular bottle with one square black cap`.
- Rear face and underside are unknown and never exposed.
- The fictional example product has no printed label; actual product printing
  would remain canonical and require inspection.
- Three plain matte miniature building blocks are incidental set pieces with
  no signage, occupants or independent movement.
- No capacity, contents, efficacy, ranking or manufacturing claim is supplied.
- Post owns one stylized tonal cue and the exact CTA `See the collection`.

Proposition: the bottle's rectangular silhouette briefly joins a skyline,
then separates through parallax so the product becomes the clear hero.
The initial apparent scale is a declared visual metaphor, not product size.

## Geometry Ledger

Use x for screen-right travel and z for depth away from the starting camera.
The camera begins south of the tabletop looking north, slightly downward.
The bottle stands in the foreground; the three blocks stand farther north.
All four objects keep base contact with the same tabletop throughout.

Choose block heights and depths so their projected tops sit just below the
bottle's cap at the starting sightline. One block is behind the bottle's left
edge, one behind its right edge and one farther to frame-right. The bottle
partly occludes the first two; projected overlap makes a continuous skyline.
These are proposed relations, not solved measurements or a proven optical layout.

| Relation | Opening | During Move | Ending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Frontal sightline, all required objects in focus | Short rightward lateral translation, orientation held, no orbit or zoom | Stop within Image 2's supported front three-quarter envelope |
| Bottle and blocks | Four rigid supported objects at fixed depths | Foreground bottle shifts left in frame more than distant blocks | Overlap opens on its right, making depth separation readable |
| Support | Bottle and block bases on tabletop | Bases and contact shadows stay visible | Same base contact, no scale or position change |
| Light | Broad high west area source, frame-left key | Fixed source, coherent shadows | Same light and exposure |

The camera move is deliberately the cause of the reveal. It must not keep the
initial alignment perfectly locked by secretly moving or resizing the blocks.
Do not add a support-removal trick, mask transition or hidden cut.

## State Contract

| Phase / Source | Approximate Window | Apparent Reading and Event | End State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A / S1 | 0-3 s | Held medium-wide table view, bottle already recognizable but apparently joining the skyline | Stable starting overlap |
| B / S1 | 3-7 s | Short rightward camera translation with fixed orientation and normal perspective; near bottle moves left relative to far blocks in projection | Depth separation becomes visible |
| C / S1 | 7-10 s | Held front three-quarter product view within supplied coverage | Four supported objects, bottle isolated enough for recognition |

The move's amount is constrained by the view evidence and useful separation,
not an invented centimeter measurement. Constant lighting prevents an unrelated
shadow change from becoming a second reveal mechanism.

## Complete Source Prompt S1

```text
References and identity:
@Image 1 defines the bottle's front identity.
@Image 2 defines its permitted front three-quarter view.
Preserve the amber rectangular bottle with one square black cap.
Keep it closed, upright and rigid with its base on the tabletop.

Staged relation:
This is generated practical-illusion-style imagery, not footage of a real shoot.
One bottle stands nearer the camera on a matte tabletop. Three plain miniature
building blocks stand farther behind it on the same surface, with their own
base contact. From the opening view, their tops project just below the cap.
One block is partly behind the bottle's left edge, one partly behind its right
edge and the third is farther frame-right. Their overlaps form an apparent
skyline around the bottle. This apparent scale is a visual metaphor.

Action and camera:
One continuous unbroken ten-second shot, no cuts or hidden joins.
0-3 seconds: hold a slightly downward frontal medium-wide view. Show the whole
bottle and skyline with enough focus depth to read near and far objects.
3-7 seconds: translate the camera a short distance to the right while holding
its orientation. The nearer bottle moves left in the image faster than the
distant blocks because of parallax. The block behind its right edge becomes
less occluded. Keep every object fixed in world position and scale.
End the move before any unsupported rear face of the bottle appears, within
the front three-quarter view established by Image 2.
7-10 seconds: hold the separated arrangement with the bottle readable as the
foreground product and all objects still resting on the same tabletop.

Visual style and light:
Photographed-looking realism, normal-lens perspective, rigid rectangular
geometry and consistent contact shadows. One broad area source fixed high on
the west side provides frame-left light throughout. The camera path stays above
the table and clear of every object. Do not resize objects to preserve overlap.
Keep the final background quiet for separately added copy.
Generate no added lettering, captions, graphic logos or CTA overlays.

Audio:
Picture-only footage for one stylized tonal accent added in post.
No native speech, music or other soundtrack.
```

## Request and Post Handoff

Request intent: one ten-second 16:9 continuous source at the requested supported
target, `generate_audio: false` where supported. Caller verification governs
duration and parameters; unsupported combinations require a decision.
Any explicitly authorized source with returned audio must be muted before post.

No geometry correction, support removal or artificial join is authorized.
Post may trim stable head/tail handles but must preserve continuous parallax.
If the scene warps, report the failed relation and propose a scoped revision;
do not present the warp as magic or silently order another source.

Place one stylized tonal accent at the observed point of separation after
playback. Exact synchronization is editorial, not guaranteed by timestamps.
Add `See the collection` in a deterministic finishing layer during the hold.
Placement safe zone is unresolved, so layout is provisional. Another ratio may
crop the skyline anchors and needs its own geometry check, not an automatic crop.

## Acceptance and Policy

Proposed checks: four objects throughout, fixed scale and support, near/far
parallax in the correct direction, coherent occlusion, known bottle views and
clear product ending. Still images cannot prove the continuous geometry.
All output and audio criteria remain untested.

The hypothetical default/approve-for-me project needs no automatic draft;
later agent selection requires actual inspection. Strict retains detailed review
and draft-first unless explicitly overridden, without silently expanding source
count. Paid-ad disclosure remains the user's separate decision. This exercise
does not prove a physically feasible rig or real capture.
