# Seedance Practical Illusion Ads

Create a product reveal whose apparent impossibility has a stated spatial
explanation. Own illusion geometry, sightline, state contract, prompt and post
handoff. Remain a prompt-only leaf: do not generate, acquire assets, load or
invoke sibling skills, render, certify engineering or publish.

## Inputs and Scope

Resolve:

- Product identity, selected views, supported facts and intended audience reaction.
- Desired apparent event and whether the mechanism should be revealed or concealed.
- Allowed geometry, camera motion, occluders, supports and compositing.
- Runtime, ratio, placement, reveal deadline and approved finishing copy.
- Explicit source generation count, native cuts or continuous-take requirement.
- Canonical cast, product and location anchors; authorized likeness if present.
- Audio route, confirmed axes, existing locks and permitted revision span.

Ask about contradictions between a locked camera path and the intended illusion.
A provisional geometry proposal is useful without references; label it and do
not invent selected bindings. Unknown product views remain unknown.
An illusion may create curiosity, but generated imagery cannot prove capacity,
strength, size, efficacy or any other unsupported product property.

## 1. Explain the Trick Before Describing the Spectacle

Connect hook, viewer curiosity, development, payoff and product role before
geometry detail. Name a plausible initial misreading and what evidence later
changes it. A product merely standing beside miniature scenery may create no
illusion to resolve. Make the reveal reinterpret the opening, not just expose
more background. If the brief locks concealment, resolve product recognition
without exposing the mechanism. Preserve a fixed sightline when it carries
the effect; camera movement is not a quality requirement.
Keep calculations in the handoff and essential staged geometry in the prompt.

Write two sentences:

```text
Apparent event = what the audience initially thinks occupies the frame.
Actual staged relation = fixed objects, planes and supports causing that reading.
```

Choose a relation that can be described without a morph:
foreground/background alignment, a partial occluder hiding separation, a
reflection showing a different path, or a rigid panel revealing a hidden object.
These are reasoning options, not a new effects catalog.

Make the product's advertising role explicit. It may resolve an ambiguous
outline or become recognizable as an occluder clears. The trick cannot change
the canonical product into a larger, stronger or functionally different object.
If a requested event has no coherent spatial explanation, propose an explicitly
synthetic concept outside this format rather than calling it practical.

Practical-looking Seedance imagery is still generated. Never claim it was
physically filmed, achieved in camera or produced without VFX. A prompt is not
a rig plan or safety assessment for an actual shoot.

## 2. Lock Geometry and Sightline

Write a geometry ledger in a single world coordinate convention:

```text
camera origin / optical axis / height / lens intent / allowed movement envelope
foreground, product and background planes / relative depth and scale
alignment anchors / projected screen positions / allowed error
occluder edge and travel / overlap order / interval of full or partial cover
support points / contact shadows / reflection or mirror plane if used
product pose and known views / geometry that must never change
```

Use qualitative relations if dimensions are unknown; do not invent measurements
and present them as solved geometry. For known dimensions, a simplified
perspective estimate can compare projected size using size divided by distance
from the camera. Label it an approximation, not a calibrated render or guarantee.

An apparent shared edge can join objects at different depths only from suitable
viewpoints. Identify the exact anchors that must align, not merely "perfect
perspective." Lock camera translation when parallax would separate them.
A zoom changes framing, not the underlying viewpoint; a lateral move changes
depth relationships. Neither should be prescribed casually.

State the lens's visible result and enough depth of field for both required
planes. Extreme blur may conceal the relation rather than establish it.
If exact projected geometry is mandatory, propose a deterministic layout or
controlled compositing handoff; Seedance is not a geometric constraint solver.

## 3. Make Support, Occlusion and Parallax Causal

Account for every object before and after the reveal. An occluded product is
already at a known resting position; it does not spawn when the mask moves.
A raised prop has a stated support even when hidden from the chosen view.
Support removal, if permitted, is a declared post task, not proof of free flight.

Define what the camera can and cannot see during each interval. The occluder
must cover the relevant contour until the intended reveal; shadow or reflection
leaks can expose hidden geometry early. Keep those relations in the prompt.
Do not solve leaks by adding inconsistent light or changing product topology.

For a parallax reveal, specify the starting alignment, the controlled camera
displacement that breaks it, and the final visible separation. Keep object
scale constant throughout. If the trick must remain concealed, keep the camera
inside the permitted sightline instead of allowing an arbitrary orbit.

For reflection, name mirror plane, reflected subject and camera exclusion zone.
Do not claim an unseen reflection is known. Reflective products and real-person
reflections require additional reference and likeness evidence where relevant.

## 4. Build Setup, Reveal and Recognition States

Hold the ambiguous relation long enough to read, change one cause, then let the
product resolve. A reveal need not disclose the trick if the brief says conceal.
Keep it product-led instead of adding unrelated optical puzzles.

Record each shot or continuous-take phase:

```text
shot_id / source_id / approximate window / apparent reading
camera sightline and movement envelope / geometry state in
one moving cause / fixed objects / support and occlusion during action
geometry state out / recognizable product anchors / reveal or conceal result
size / angle / lens intent / camera start-path-end
named light source, world position and key side / shadows and reflections
continuity boundary / handles / sound cue / native or post owner
```

Explain static camera and constant light choices; here they often preserve
alignment and prevent the hidden setup from being exposed accidentally.
Retain confirmed axes and existing shot-plan decisions. Where they conflict
with the trick, ask which constraint may change; do not silently replace them.

One source generation is not necessarily one take. Preserve explicit source
count and place allowed native shots inside those sources. An explicit
continuous take has no concealed joins, reset cuts or object substitution.
Do not add generations, retries, drafts or coverage to rescue geometry.
Unsupported duration or overloaded staging needs a decision, not silent splitting.
Model timing is approximate; frame-exact reveals and cuts require editorial
control and adequate source handles.

## 5. Write Prompt and Post Handoff

Provide complete subject, action, environment, visual style, camera and audio
direction per source. Put the real staged relation before the apparent effect.
State what stays rigid, which plane moves, and the end-state geometry.
Bind selected references by ordered role; boards and diagrams remain planning
artifacts, not automatic identity references.

Keep request intent separate: count, duration, ratio, supported target and
audio behavior. The caller checks live capabilities. For silence or post-only
audio, specify `generate_audio: false` when supported; mute/discard audio from
an explicitly authorized alternative before adding the post mix.
Sound can accent the reveal but must not imply an unsupported mechanism.

Exact overlay copy, logo, captions and CTA belong to deterministic post.
Canonical printed product branding remains subject to actual inspection against
acquired references. Do not replace it with an invented or erased mark.
An exact product layer requires correct perspective, holdout masks, support and
shadows throughout; a correct closing card cannot repair earlier identity drift.

Give post the alignment anchors, permissible crops, occlusion intervals,
declared support-removal/compositing tasks, trim handles and soundtrack ownership.
A crop may conceal a support but must not break the product recognition or
verified placement safe zone. Unknown platform margins remain unknown.
Identify which illusion relations cannot survive reframing to another ratio.
Do not claim inspected timing, geometry or audio when only a plan exists.

## 6. Return and Revise

Return apparent event, staged explanation, evidence gaps, geometry ledger,
shot/source plan, complete prompts, request intent, post handoff and acceptance
criteria. A scoped repair adjusts the named cause or interval and preserves
other locks. If a locked transition cannot work, request a decision before
substituting a cut or an exact composite.

Inherit current independent `review_mode` and `approval_mode`. Absent fields
mean `default` and `approve_for_me` for new actions only; invalid values block
production. Default uses minimal preflight and actual final sanity; strict
retains detailed review. Every new Seedance 2.5 generation requires
`resolution: "480p"` and boolean `draft: true` in both review modes.
Promote only selected native Drafts. Surface source-count conflicts before
generation; never bypass drafts or silently expand scope. Prompt-only work
generates nothing.
`approve_for_me` permits inspected in-scope agent selection;
`ask_for_approval` waits for the user. Neither grants consent, budget or release.
Paid-ad disclosure remains the user's recorded decision. Prompt-only work
needs no production locks and cannot approve uninspected media.

Acceptance checks cover fixed geometry, sightline, parallax, overlap order,
support, stable product identity and a readable reveal. Playback is needed for
temporal validity; stills cannot prove a continuous illusion. Leave unknown
results untested. Invent no real-capture claim, testimonial, efficacy evidence,
sales ranking, conversion promise or platform guarantee.

## Composition Hints

The caller may pair `seedance-shot-design` and axis specialists with coverage,
`seedance-prompt-25` with grammar and deterministic finishing with exact geometry.
Free magical morphs belong to synthetic treatments; source footage edits belong
to editing capabilities. These are prose hints, not sibling loading dependencies.
This leaf owns spatial illusion logic, not an effects or camera catalog.
