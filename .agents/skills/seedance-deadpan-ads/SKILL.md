# Seedance Deadpan Ads

Make the mismatch between a serious response and a disproportionate situation
read without announcing the joke. Own understated performance, observational
camera grammar and comic information timing. Remain an independent prompt-only
leaf; never generate, acquire, render, publish or change production state.

## Scope and inputs

Use a mundane procedure treated too seriously, an absurd rule accepted as
ordinary, or a quiet discrepancy between speech and visible behavior.
Do not flatten every short story into deadpan. A plot-led emotional reversal
without understated performance or documentary observation belongs elsewhere.
Mockumentary is a fictional treatment, not actual customer testimony.

Read supplied material and establish:

- Comic premise, target of the joke, desired restraint and forbidden targets.
- Product identity, supported facts, limitations and intended next action.
- Audience, language/register, setting and any cultural sensitivities supplied.
- Cast, roles, status relationships, character knowledge and rights.
- Documentary device, if requested, and whether the camera is acknowledged.
- Runtime, ratio, reveal deadline, placement, generation count and take/cut locks.
- Exact dialogue, audio route, CTA, overlays and current selected references.
- Revision scope, review_mode, approval_mode and existing creative decisions.

Ask about missing facts that materially affect the joke or representation.
Offer a clearly hypothetical premise when exploration is requested, never
invented brand canon, real customer anecdotes or a claimed cultural norm.
Do not assume a country, accent, workplace hierarchy or brand personality.
Read [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md) for a hypothetical
worked package and when testing or changing this skill.

## 1. Build one playable incongruity

Connect hook, viewer curiosity, development, payoff and product role before
performance restraint. Make the situation disproportionate enough to read
without a funny voice: scale, social ritual, status or a visible contradiction
can carry absurdity. Solemnly handling a small object is not by itself a joke.
Test setup, recognition and release; each should contribute something new.
Keep performances sincere and task-directed, not blank. Do not solve a weak
premise with laughter, a sting or frantic cuts. Carry the actual comic action
into the prompt; keep compliance and task bookkeeping in the handoff.

Write `ordinary task / disproportionate rule or response / visible contradiction`.
Choose one primary comic mechanism rather than stacking unrelated gags.

| Mechanism | Performable event | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procedural overkill | A character uses elaborate ceremony for a tiny task | Invented safety certification or technical requirements |
| Status mismatch | The person acting important is quietly unnecessary | Cruelty toward a vulnerable person as the only joke |
| Literal acceptance | A character earnestly follows an absurd fictional rule | Implying the real product requires the absurd rule |
| Observed contradiction | The picture quietly contradicts a solemn statement | An unsupported performance comparison masquerading as proof |
| Delayed recognition | A character notices what the audience already sees | Repeated reaction shots that explain the same joke |

Keep the target legible: a fictional procedure, vanity or disproportionate
response, not an unsubstantiated allegation about a real person or company.
An invented expert, laboratory or news crew must not imply genuine authority.
Define which absurdities are fiction and which product behavior stays factual.

Connect the product to the action: its supported operation can make a solemn
procedure unnecessary, or reveal the status mismatch. Do not attach a packshot
to an unrelated sketch and assume the brand connection will follow.
Honor reveal deadlines even when a later reveal would make an easier joke.
No format is inherently funnier or higher-converting across markets.

## 2. Direct sincerity, not blankness

Give each performer a practical intention and a belief that makes the behavior
reasonable to them. Low expression is not absence of thought.
Direct the visible footprint: fixed attention on the task, a measured breath,
a small correction of posture, a brief check of the other person's reaction.
Choose one or two cues; a list of microexpressions becomes overacting.

Specify what changes at the comic event and what does not. A character can
register new information with an eye movement while continuing the same task.
Avoid automatic smirks, winks, eyebrow flourishes, comedic voices and laughter.
Do not remove an explicitly requested smile or direct-address moment; reconcile
it with the intended level of restraint.

```text
beat_id / ordinary task / comic mismatch / target / fictional boundary
character belief and intention / audience knowledge / character knowledge
trigger event / attention target / visible cue / response withheld
state_before -> state_after / product role / claim_ids
pause purpose / release event / camera knowledge and behavior
```

The audience may know first, the camera may discover late, or a cut may reveal
the discrepancy. Select one information order. Do not prescribe a reaction
before the performer could see or hear the trigger.
Treat a pause as time to notice or decide, not dead air of a fixed magic length.
Test proposed timing with a read or existing performance when available.

## 3. Choose an observational camera contract

Use a documentary device only when it serves the premise:
an off-axis interview, a fixed observational wide, an operator's delayed
reframe, or an insert of the absurd procedure. Stable camera work can be
documentary-like; wobble, crash zooms and poor exposure are not prerequisites.

```text
shot_id / source_id / approximate window / documentary device
camera acknowledged or unacknowledged / operator knows what and when
subject position / eyeline / action / trigger / reaction / end state
size / angle / lens intent / camera start -> path -> end / reason
named light source / key side / quality / static reason
reference roles / prop count and positions / audio / transition and handles
```

For a delayed reframe, state the event the operator notices, the delay's
purpose, direction and final subject. Do not ask for simultaneous anticipation
and surprise. An interview eyeline stays on the interviewer unless an explicit
camera glance is the joke. In one take, blocking or focus can reveal information
without introducing prohibited cuts.

Use credible spatial access and motivated practical light. Keep product action
visible and preserve prop possession across interviews and cutaways.
Any deliberate discontinuity must be named, not used to excuse a missing prop.

## 4. Keep timing and sound controllable

Separate setup, recognition and release in the editorial plan. Allow the
viewer to see the discrepancy before cutting away; avoid explaining it twice.
Silence, room tone and a small material sound can support restraint, but honor
requested music. Do not add a laugh track, sting or comic zoom by convention.
Record exact authorized dialogue, language, speaker and pause purpose.

Preserve requested generation count and continuous take. One source generation
may contain native cuts when allowed; it does not authorize multiple sources.
Durations and reference roles are verified at runtime by the caller.
Prompt timing is approximate. Frame-exact reaction holds, cut delays, musical
hits and retiming belong to post, not promised native model behavior.
Plan handles around a pause without claiming a fixed duration guarantees humor.
If speech and the joke do not fit, propose an authorized shorter setup or
runtime change; do not silently remove words or add generations.

## 5. Separate fiction, evidence and exact graphics

Maintain `claim_id / source / conditions / wording / status / affected beat`.
Inspect factual implications in both the sincere dialogue and absurd picture.
No unsupported efficacy, rankings, certificates, savings or real before/after
results. Generated scenes illustrate a premise, never provide measured proof.
Do not invent customer histories or verified-buyer badges for interview scenes.
A synthetic performer is an actor or presenter, not an actual customer.

Respect rights for likeness, voice, brand references, source footage and music.
Parody styling alone does not grant rights to a real person's identity or a
specific program's assets. Do not disguise privacy-rejected references.
Source-ad timing claims need inspected footage, not recollection or a transcript.

Keep all added overlays, CTA, logos, captions, mock lower-thirds and UI exact
in deterministic post. Fictional job titles must not imply real credentials.
Inspect printed product labels against authorized references throughout;
exact product fidelity may need real imagery or compositing.
Use actual placement templates and leave unknown safe zones unresolved.
Disclosure requirements remain distinct from comic labels and creative choices;
the user's paid-delivery decision is not delegated to a fictional narrator.

## 6. Return, revise and check

Deliver premise and fiction boundary, beat/shot/source records, claim/rights
gaps and a complete prompt per requested source. The prompt contains subject
bindings, literal actions and cues, environment, visual style/light, camera
behavior, exact speech and audio. "Make it deadpan" is not a complete prompt.
Keep unresolved bindings outside the prompt and label drafts provisional.

Separate request intent: model, ratio, supported duration, count, native cuts,
ordered reference roles and audio route. For silent/post-audio work specify
`generate_audio: false` when supported; an authorized alternative returning
audio needs explicit mute/discard before mixing. Native speech needs listening.
The caller resolves actual tool parameters; this leaf never submits them.

Return edit windows, trigger/reaction handles, sound ownership, exact post copy,
CTA destination, disclosure status and placement-template evidence.
For a narrow revision identify whether the defect is premise clarity,
performance intensity, information order or cut timing. Change only that layer
and its dependencies. A request for a smaller reaction is not permission to
rewrite dialogue, replace the cast, add cutaways or regenerate the whole ad.

Check that the trigger precedes reaction, eyelines match knowledge, the
contradiction is visible, the performer remains task-directed, the product
operates as supported and the release does not explain the joke again.
Use playback for pause/camera behavior and listening for delivery/sound.
Report pass, fail or untested with evidence; a frame cannot prove comic timing.
Audience amusement remains a hypothesis until tested, not a QA pass claim.

Honor review_mode independently of approval_mode. Prompt-only work generates
nothing; checks and production remain caller-owned.
Every new Seedance 2.5 generation requires `resolution: "480p"` and boolean
`draft: true` in both review modes. Promote only selected native Drafts.
Surface source-count conflicts before generation; never bypass drafts or
silently expand scope.
Optional caller composition may use film-production for execution,
seedance-prompt-25 for model grammar, and acting, camera or exact-graphics
specialists. These are prose hints, never sibling commands or dependencies.
