# Worked Example: A Line in Motion

Hypothetical prompt-only exercise. No media was generated, inspected or approved.
The references and decisions below are assumed supplied solely for this example;
real bindings require actual selected files and authorization.

## Creative contrast before construction

The front-view exercise below protects limited garment evidence; it is not the
default fashion performance. For an open brief with the same front-view limits,
propose "refuse the line": the wearer stands aligned with a wall recess, then
travels laterally across three hard-edged pools of light while facing forward,
stopping deliberately off-center. Shoulders initiate the movement and sleeves
follow; attitude comes from choosing a position, not a neutral pose.

Quiet version: hold a full figure as the wearer takes one decisive lateral step
away from the imposed alignment and meets the lens. Expressive version: a
front-facing rhythmic three-step phrase, cut to a low frontal silhouette, then
a planted off-center finish. Both preserve the evidenced jacket construction;
neither exposes unknown rear views, promises comfort or invents fabric content.
The changed lighting and choreography are proposals for an open brief, not
permission to replace the locked window light in the exercise below.

**Weak/repair test:** Weight shift -> short step -> blank hold is insufficient
when the brief asks for attitude. Add a motivated physical phrase, not an orbit
that hides the garment or a compulsory wind machine.

## Brief and Authority

A ten-second 4:5 editorial ad for a slate-blue jacket. The requested reaction is
attention to the vertical front seam as the wearer moves, not a fit recommendation.
Exactly one source generation with three native shots is requested, not a
continuous take. Post owns a supplied instrumental track and the exact CTA
`Explore the collection`. No voice, price, destination URL or benefit is invented.

- Image 2 is the selected front garment photo.
- Image 3 is a selected front three-quarter wearer view of the same jacket.
- Canonical descriptor is `slate-blue hip-length jacket with two patch pockets,
  three dark front buttons and straight sleeves`.
- The selected wearer styling shows the same plain trousers and shoes in all

Proposition: a narrow vertical architectural recess echoes the jacket's front
seam; one step interrupts the alignment and a still finish restores attention
to the garment. The set is editorial staging, not evidence of product origin.

## Continuity and Shot Contract

World convention: wearer faces south toward the camera. A broad window west of
the wearer is the fixed key, reading frame-left. Cameras remain south of the
wearer. Constant light lets the cloth be compared across views.

Native cuts are approximate. Source count remains one even if their timing varies.

## Complete Source Prompt S1

```text
References:
@Image 1 defines the invented adult wearer's identity, build, hair and styling.
@Image 2 defines the garment, its front construction and colour.
@Image 3 defines the same wearer and garment at the permitted three-quarter angle.
Preserve the slate-blue hip-length jacket with two patch pockets, three dark
front buttons and straight sleeves. Keep the jacket fully buttoned, sleeves
down, and the selected trousers and shoes unchanged.

Subject and scene:
One wearer stands before a plain plaster wall with a narrow vertical recess.
The recess echoes the jacket's front seam without touching or obscuring it.
This is an AI-generated editorial fashion treatment, with natural body and cloth
behavior, not a fit simulation or a record of a real shoot.

Action and camera:
Three native shots in one approximately ten-second source.
0-3 seconds: level, front full-figure view, normal-lens perspective. The wearer
stands facing the camera with both hands at their sides, then shifts weight
onto the left foot. Keep the full hem and shoes visible. Hold the camera so
the silhouette and fastening state are readable.
3-7 seconds: cut to a knees-up front three-quarter view within Image 3's known
coverage. The right foot takes one short step toward the camera while the
shoulders remain nearly frontal. The camera tracks slightly backward to retain
framing. Both hands stay away from the pockets. The sleeve folds follow the
arms; the jacket hem lags behind the step and then settles at its original length.
7-10 seconds: cut to a front head-to-knee view after the foot plants. Hold the
camera. The wearer relaxes both arms and remains still. The same two pockets,
three buttons, seam paths and straight sleeves remain visible and unchanged.

Visual style and light:
Photographed-looking editorial realism with faithful slate-blue colour.
One broad window fixed west of the wearer is the soft frame-left key in all
views. Preserve contact shadows and readable fabric texture. The wall and
light remain unchanged. Stay on front and supported front three-quarter views.
Keep the lower background visually quiet for a separately added CTA.
Generate no added lettering, captions, graphic logos or CTA overlays.

Audio:
Picture-only source for a supplied instrumental track added in post.
No generated dialogue or soundtrack.
```

## Request and Post Handoff

Request intent: one ten-second 4:5 source, requested supported delivery target,
`generate_audio: false` where supported. Duration/target support is caller-verified;
an unsupported combination requires a decision, not extra sources.
An explicitly authorized route returning sound must have that track muted.

The hypothetical project uses default review with approve-for-me for later
inspected selections. It does not require a draft. Strict review would retain
its detailed checks and draft default unless overridden by the explicit scope;
neither policy authorizes unseen output or extra generations.

Post may trim within the ten-second source and move cuts only where stable
handles exist. Keep the step and cloth settling intact. Do not create exact
cut times by warping the garment or holding an awkward mid-step frame.
No soundtrack has been measured; align supplied music accents only after picture
selection. Use no unrequested foley.

Add `Explore the collection` as exact deterministic text during the final hold.
Placement is unspecified, so layout stays provisional until its safe zone is
verified. No graphic logo was supplied. Inspect any acquired on-garment printing
in the actual output; do not invent or erase branding. Paid delivery needs the
user's disclosure decision separately.

## Acceptance and Limits

Proposed checks: garment recognizable in A, stable fit and topology through B,
no unsupported rear view, one readable finishing state, no claims or fake
endorsement. Playback must establish cloth causality and continuity; a contact
sheet is insufficient. All output checks remain untested in this exercise.
