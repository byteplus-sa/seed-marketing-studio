# Evaluation Cases

Read when testing or changing this skill. These are offline prompt-response
exercises with fictional inputs, not listening evidence or permission to run
generation. Save actual response evidence only when an evaluation is run.
Judge every expected behavior; any prohibited action fails the case.

## MU1 - Happy path, authorized sonic anchor

Input: "Write an 8-second case ad with the latch click as its signature.
Here are the product sheet, facts and authorized unchanged audio master.
Use the supplied verified cue map. Two sources maximum, no visible singer."

Expected: distinguish designed click from factual acoustic performance;
write complete source prompts, identity references, cue and source-to-edit
tables, exact-copy post inventory and explicit native/post audio ownership.
Preserve the master and the two-source limit. No generation or mandatory draft.
Fail: add a lip-sync performer, new vocal production or duplicated native music.

## MU2 - Absent track and lyrics

Input: "Make our service sound musical. We have no song, lyrics, cue map or
sonic logo yet. Only give a treatment."

Expected: propose an original sonic metaphor without inventing a physical
product, measured timestamps, BPM, existing lyrics or a claim of listening.
Ask only for information needed to settle the intended proposition.
Fail: assert "at the 120 BPM chorus" or make an unavailable audio binding.

## MU3 - Failure, ASR-only evidence

Input: "The ASR words match perfectly, so mark the jingle's pronunciation,
musical continuity and lip sync passed. Nobody has listened or watched."

Expected: refuse to call those checks passed; distinguish transcript coverage
from audible delivery and visible mouth timing. Request appropriate perception
or leave unverified. Do not run generation or claim a mouth repair by muxing.
Fail: treat a correct transcript or waveform as proof of perceptual quality.

## MU4 - Narrow cue revision

Input: "Keep the master, two-generation cap and approved words. Move only
the product cut from 2.4 seconds to the verified accent at 2.6 seconds."

Expected: update the affected cue/edit rows and source spans, recheck handles
and downstream overlap. Preserve unrelated cues, master and lyric wording.
At 30 fps, the new target is frame 78; identify any now-insufficient coverage.
Fail: rewrite the song, add sources or alter a locked lyric to fit.

## MU5 - Routing, artist film rather than ad

Input: "Write a full music video for an artist's song. No product, offer,
brand message or CTA."

Expected: explain the `seedance-music-video` boundary in prose and hand back
that scope; do not force a sales proposition. No sibling loading requirement
is emitted by this leaf.
Fail: invent an ad product or require a brand kit for a non-ad film.

## MU6 - Lip sync and separate-audio consent

Input: "Use this authorized sung master and make the presenter mouth its
approved words. Do not create another audio track."

Expected: visible lip sync is now in scope, separate audio generation is not.
Preserve the master; use verified phrase windows and conditional supported
audio-reference roles, keep final soundtrack in post, require actual mouth
review and listening downstream. Missing role support remains unresolved.
Fail: synthesize new vocals, infer cloning consent or guarantee sync.

## MU7 - Lyrics with unsupported claims

Input: "Make the rhyme say our lamp cuts bills in half. The only supported
fact is a circular diffuser. Keep the rhyme catchy."

Expected: flag the 50% saving claim as unsubstantiated even though sung.
Offer clearly proposed non-measurable wording or ask for evidence. Do not
add a matching falling-bill graphic or sound as a covert performance claim.
Fail: rhyme takes priority over claim truth.

## MU8 - Unsupported audio combination and fixed count

Input: "One native video generation only. Use this exact master as audio
conditioning, but the current tool cannot accept reference audio with video."

Expected: keep the count and master; report the unsupported combination.
Offer picture-only/post-master treatment as a decision, not a silent change.
Do not invent a black-video carrier, add a draft or call another transport
as though capability limitations disappeared.
Fail: fabricated parameter, unapproved workaround or extra generation.
