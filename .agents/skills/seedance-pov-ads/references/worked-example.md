## Brief and Authority

Eight-second 16:9 eye-level first-person ad for a plain blue handled mug.
Exactly one continuous source, no cuts or retries. The participant notices the
handle, lifts the mug a short distance and sets it on a coaster. The proposition
is a legible handling action, not proof of comfort, heat resistance or quality.
No liquid, drinking, speech, testimonial, logo or CTA is requested.

- Image 1: selected three-quarter mug view showing its exterior and handle.
- Image 2: selected raised three-quarter view showing the same empty interior.
- Canonical descriptor: `one matte blue cylindrical mug with one rounded handle`.
- The brief supplies authorized generic adult hand appearance, right-handed
  action and an unmirrored view. No identifiable real-person reference is used.
- A plain cork coaster is incidental set dressing, not a second product.
- The underside of the mug is unknown and must never be shown.

## Complete Source Prompt S1

```text
Viewpoint and references:
One continuous unbroken eight-second shot, no cuts or concealed joins.
The camera is at a seated participant's eye position, looking gently downward
at a table. It remains attached to that viewpoint, not to the chest or mug.
The image is unmirrored. @Image 1 defines the exterior and handle of the mug.
@Image 2 defines the same mug's visible empty interior.
Preserve one matte blue cylindrical mug with one rounded handle.

Subject and environment:
A quiet tabletop with its far edge visible. One mug begins slightly left of
frame centre with its handle on frame-right. One plain cork coaster rests
right of centre within comfortable reach. The mug has no liquid.
Use the supplied generic adult hand appearance. No other person is visible.

Action:
Approximately 0-2 seconds, hold on the mug and coaster with both hands below
frame. The mug rests flat on the table.
Approximately 2-4 seconds, the participant's anatomical right hand enters from
frame-right and reaches the handle. Fingers curl through the handle and the
thumb rests outside. The anatomical left hand remains below frame and does
not touch anything. Form a stable grip before lifting.
Approximately 4-6 seconds, the right hand lifts the mug only enough to clear
the tabletop, moves it rightward to the coaster and lowers its base flat onto
the coaster. Keep the same handle orientation, rigid shape and empty interior.
Approximately 6-8 seconds, release only after the base is supported. The right
hand withdraws frame-right. End with the single mug resting on the single coaster.

Camera, style and light:
Natural eye-position perspective, medium-close view of the task. Keep both
the starting point and coaster visible with enough focus depth to read contact.
Hold the camera and its downward angle throughout. The mug stays upright and
below the optical axis; never reveal its underside or pass through a hand.
Photographed-looking generated imagery with plausible anatomy, wrist alignment
and contact shadows. A broad window fixed west of the table is the frame-left
key; the east wall gives soft fill. Keep light and tabletop landmarks stable.
Generate no added lettering, captions, graphic logos or CTA overlays.

Audio:
Picture-only footage. A restrained stylized contact cue will be added in post.
No generated speech, breath, music or other soundtrack.
```

## Request and Post Handoff

Request intent: one eight-second 16:9 source at the requested supported target,
continuous take, `generate_audio: false` when supported. Verify duration and
parameter support through the caller. Mute returned audio only under an
explicitly authorized alternative; do not accidentally mix it with post sound.

Preserve the entire reach/contact/release chain. Do not insert a cut or speed
change to hide an impossible grip. The approximate phase boundaries are not
frame-exact instructions. A stylized ceramic-contact cue may coincide with
observed base contact after picture selection; it is not an authentic recording
of this product. No track timing has been measured here.

No overlays are requested. Later supplied CTA or logo would use deterministic
post with a verified placement safe zone, without covering the handle or target.
If actual product printing is supplied, preserve and inspect it rather than
treating this example's plain finish as a default.

## Acceptance and Policy

Proposed checks: one unbroken eye viewpoint, right hand only, no view of unknown
underside, stable mug count, grip before lift, support before release and clear
final recognition. Motion requires playback and sound requires listening.
These remain untested, not passed.

The hypothetical default review/ask-for-approval project needs no automatic
draft, but later inspected selection waits for the user. Strict review would
retain its detailed review and draft default unless explicitly overridden;
the caller must preserve the explicit one-source scope. Rights and any paid-ad
disclosure decision remain separate from creative approval.
