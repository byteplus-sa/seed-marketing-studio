# Seedance Handcrafted Ads

Make a constructed material action explain a product idea. Own the ad's
material roles, pose-state sequence, complete prompt and post handoff.
Remain a prompt-only leaf. Do not generate, acquire media, load or invoke
sibling skills, render, certify physical capture or publish.

## Inputs and Scope

Resolve:

- Product identity and selected views, supported facts and intended action.
- Audience reaction and the one visual relationship to communicate.
- Requested craft medium and whether the product itself may be stylized.
- Canonical product, cast and setting anchors that may not change.
- Runtime, ratio, placement, recognition deadline and exact finishing copy.
- Intended pose cadence, requested delivery FPS if known and motion smoothness.
- Source generation count, native cuts or continuous-take constraint.
- Audio route, confirmed axes and permissible revision scope.

Do not assume "handcrafted" means stepped motion. Tactile digital animation can
move smoothly; stop-motion-inspired work can use deliberate discrete poses.
Ask which distinction matters when unspecified. Label any provisional treatment
and leave unavailable references unbound rather than inventing selected files.

Distinguish a Seedance-simulated handcrafted look from documented physical
stop-motion capture. Neither texture nor stepped motion proves objects were
built, photographed or handmade. A request for true capture is outside this
prompt leaf; do not relabel generated footage as filmed craft.

## 1. Assign Material Roles

Connect hook, viewer motivation, development, payoff and product role before
pose bookkeeping. Make the material do something conceptually useful: unfold
a journey, build a relationship, resist then yield, or reconfigure a world.
Paper texture plus two panels uncovering a product is not automatically a
selling idea. Anticipation and material consequence can be compelling at a
quiet pace; neither jitter nor more cuts creates inventiveness.
Keep counts and construction ledgers in the handoff, carrying the essential
support, material behavior and identity constraints into the prompt.

Choose one selling idea that can be expressed by arranging, folding, stacking,
rolling or revealing tangible-looking pieces. Start with the relationship
between the product and those pieces, not a catalog of animation media.

Declare the product's role explicitly:

| Role | What can change | What stays fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canonical hero in a crafted set | Surrounding paper, wool or other declared set material moves | Product identity, parts, surface and supported operation |
| Authorized stylized replica | Approved representation and material treatment | Agreed silhouette, proportions, components and identity anchors |
| Symbolic stand-in | A clearly described metaphor carries an intangible idea | No implication that the metaphor proves product performance |

Do not convert the product into clay just because the background is clay.
When an exact product is embedded in a stylized world, name the boundary and
keep it stable. An approved stylized replica is not proof of actual material
composition or a license to invent unseen views.

Choose two or three material cues with an advertising job: visible paper edges
can frame a thin silhouette; a folded backdrop can reveal a known product face.
Avoid an inventory of textures competing with recognition.

## 2. Engineer Constructible State Changes

Write a construction ledger before the timeline:

```text
element_id / product, set or metaphor role / reference authority
material and thickness impression / seams, joints or layer order
rigid parts / permitted bending or compression / support and contact
piece count / entry and exit positions / allowed replacement states
surface anchors / unknown surfaces or views / forbidden identity changes
```

Each action follows the chosen construction. Rigid pieces rotate at joints;
paper bends at a named fold; soft material compresses only where permitted.
Do not use a generic melt to connect unrelated poses.
Replacement-like animation needs an explicit state correspondence: the same
piece, at the same support point, in a new designed pose. It is not spontaneous
multiplication or loss of parts.

Keep the product readable while the set moves. Reveal through an opening or
arrangement rather than covering the label with arbitrary debris.
Choose a stable tabletop landmark and coherent miniature scale. A shadow or
contact point helps establish weight even in a deliberately stylized world.
Keep surface marks anchored to their object rather than crawling every frame.

## 3. Separate Pose Cadence From Delivery FPS

Describe temporal intent in three layers:

```text
pose cadence intent / anticipation, move, settle and hold
camera cadence / continuous or stepped, independently stated
delivery FPS / exact hold and retiming ownership in post
```

"Roughly twelve pose changes per second" is an aesthetic target, not an output
FPS parameter or a guarantee of unique poses. At 24 FPS, exact two-frame holds
would produce twelve pose updates per second, but this arithmetic does not make
Seedance obey them. At other delivery rates, choose an explicit editorial
exposure pattern rather than promising the same integer hold length.

Use longer anticipation and recognition holds around short movement bursts.
Distinguish deliberate stepping from defects: random camera shake, flickering
exposure, texture boil and product wobble are not necessary craft evidence.
If exact cadence is required, hand off frame selection/repetition or authored
animation to post. Frame duplication cannot repair missing semantic poses;
optical flow may erase the requested stepped look or warp the product.

## 4. Plan Ad States and Source Boundaries

Use setup -> material action -> product recognition -> settled composition
where useful. These are functions, not a compulsory four-shot template.
An opening can already show the product; the payoff may reveal its outline
more clearly rather than pretend to manufacture it.

Record each shot or continuous-take phase:

```text
shot_id / source_id / approximate window / ad purpose
piece count and positions in / primary construction action / state out
product visibility / support, occlusion and identity invariants
size / angle / lens intent / camera start-path-end and cadence
named light source, world position and key side / surface evidence
pose rhythm / stable handle / native or editorial boundary / sound owner
```

State why a fixed camera or constant light helps compare successive states.
Retain confirmed axes and existing shot-plan decisions. A material transition
cannot silently change garment topology, human likeness or branded geometry.
Unknown reverse faces remain unknown; restrict rotations or request evidence.

Keep source generation count independent of shot and pose counts. Native cuts
may share one source if requested. An explicit continuous take remains unbroken;
stepped object motion is not permission for editorial cuts or hidden resets.
Do not add generations, retries or drafts to obtain missing poses.
Surface unsupported duration or cadence conflicts for a decision.
Prompt windows are approximate; frame-exact timing and edit handles belong to
the post plan and inspected sources.

## 5. Write Prompt and Post Handoff

Provide full subject, action, environment, visual style, camera and audio
direction for each source. Name the simulated medium, product role, construction
rules and pose-state sequence inside the prompt. Bind actual selected references
by ordered role; boards remain planning artifacts, not identity references.

Separate request intent into source count, duration, ratio, supported target and
audio behavior. Caller verification governs current parameters. For silence or
post-only audio, specify `generate_audio: false` when supported; mute/discard
audio from an explicitly authorized alternative before adding the post mix.
Describe paper, joint or tabletop sounds as stylized cues unless recordings
actually establish their provenance. Do not imply real on-set foley.

All added text, logos, captions and CTA use deterministic post layers.
Inspect canonical printed product branding against acquired references even
inside a crafted set. Do not erase or redesign it to make the material uniform.
If exact identity cannot survive generation, propose approved imagery with
proper perspective, contact shadows and occlusion, not a guaranteed recreation.
Exact copy never belongs to generated paper letters or stitched text.

Give post stable source handles, intended exposure rhythm, exact delivery FPS,
permitted retiming, sound ownership and copy placement against verified safe
zones. Unknown FPS or margins remain open. Preserve a readable ending.
Do not claim beat sync until sound and selected picture have been measured.

## 6. Return and Evaluate

Return the proposition, material-role decision, evidence gaps, construction
ledger, shot/source plan, complete prompts, request intent and post notes.
Scoped revisions change the requested material, cadence or beat only; explain
dependencies before changing the canonical product or other locked direction.

Inherit current independent `review_mode` and `approval_mode`. Absent fields
mean `default` and `approve_for_me` for new actions only; invalid values block
production. Default has minimal preflight and actual final sanity; strict
retains detailed review. Every new Seedance 2.5 generation requires
`resolution: "480p"` and boolean `draft: true` in both review modes.
Promote only selected native Drafts. Surface source-count conflicts before
generation; never bypass drafts or silently expand scope. Prompt-only work
generates nothing.
Inspected in-scope agent selection belongs to `approve_for_me`;
`ask_for_approval` waits for the user. Neither grants consent, generation scope or release.
Paid-ad disclosure remains a user-recorded decision. Prompt drafts need no
production locks and never constitute approval of unseen output.

Check construction causality, stable counts, material boundaries, product
recognition, texture stability and intended pose rhythm. Playback is necessary;
a contact sheet cannot establish cadence. Leave unobserved results untested.
Generated craft is not evidence of efficacy, sustainable production, artisan
origin or product manufacture. Invent no testimonials, sales rankings, real
capture claims, conversion gains or platform guarantees.

## Composition Hints

The caller may pair `seedance-animation-styles` with material wording,
`seedance-pacing-presets` with rhythm, `seedance-prompt-25` with grammar, and
finishing capabilities with exact cadence and graphics. Footage restyling is
a different capability. These are prose hints only, not sibling dependencies.
This leaf owns the ad's material-state argument, not an animation style catalog.
