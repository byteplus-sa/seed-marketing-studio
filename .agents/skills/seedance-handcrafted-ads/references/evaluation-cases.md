# Offline Evaluation Cases

Use these synthetic requests without generating media. Assess the proposed
material logic and handoff, not just keyword presence. Model reliability cannot
be established by these documentation checks.

## 1. Happy Path: Canonical Hero and Paper Set

Input: selected tin photographs, paper panels folding away, nine seconds,
one continuous source and a post-only CTA.

Pass: unchanged product role, panel count, fold/support locations, sequential
states and readable end are carried into a complete prompt. Cadence, camera,
light and exact post copy have separate ownership.
Fail: tin melts into paper, panels spawn or added text is generated.

## 2. Missing Material and Temporal Intent

Input: "Make this look handmade" with product photos but no requested movement.

Pass: clarify stepped stop-motion versus smoothly moving tactile animation
when consequential, offer a provisional material-role treatment and leave
runtime/cadence unresolved rather than inventing approved settings.
Fail: automatically impose twelve FPS, clay or a full material conversion.

## 3. Edge: Pose Cadence Versus FPS

Input: "Twelve poses per second, exact two-frame holds, deliver at 25 FPS."

Pass: identify that two-frame holds at 25 FPS yield 12.5 updates per second;
offer a changed delivery rate or an explicit uneven exposure pattern in post,
subject to the user's choice. Do not claim native pose guarantees.
Fail: set a made-up model FPS field or say these constraints are equivalent.

## 4. Failure: Defects Mistaken for Craft

Input: a take reportedly has a smooth moving product, boiling textures and
random camera shake; only a contact sheet is available.

Pass: distinguish intended object stepping from texture/camera defects, request
playback for cadence and propose a scoped repair preserving canonical identity.
Fail: call every irregularity authentic handmade charm or pass motion from stills.

## 5. Scoped Revision: Smooth Camera, Stepped Props

Input: "Keep the felt props stepped but make only the camera movement smooth.
Preserve product, timing, copy, source count and colour."

Pass: change camera cadence only, retain object poses and all other locks;
flag any new parallax or crop consequences without silently changing the scene.
Fail: smooth the whole clip with optical flow or redesign the felt world.

## 6. Routing: Capture, Style Block and Source Restyle

Input A: commission real physical stop-motion capture.
Input B: write only a wood-puppet material style phrase.
Input C: redraw an existing clip in felt while preserving its motion.

Pass: A remains a physical-production request, not claimed generated capture;
B stays an axis-only capability; C is source restyling. Offer prose capability
hints without loading siblings or generating anything.
Fail: force all three through a new ad sequence or claim a rig was built.

## 7. Product Evidence and Unknown Views

Input: exact branded tin from a front photo, orbit it in a clay world and say
"handmade from sustainable materials."

Pass: preserve canonical product separately from the clay set, flag missing
rear evidence and unsupported origin/environmental claims, inspect printing in
actual footage. Offer constrained framing or authorized exact-image treatment.
Fail: clay-convert the tin, invent its rear logo or use the medium as claim proof.

## 8. Generation Count, Continuous Take and Modes

Input A: two multi-shot sources, default review and ask-for-approval.
Input B: one continuous source, strict review, approve-for-me and explicit no-draft.

Pass: keep source counts distinct from shot/pose counts; A reserves later
selection for the user; B keeps no cuts and surfaces the no-draft conflict
before production, preserving native 480p boolean draft:true and the source cap.
Neither automatically adds coverage or grants rights/disclosure decisions.
Fail: generate per pose, use hidden reset cuts or treat strict as extra budget.

## 9. Exact Graphics and Paid Delivery

Input: stitch the exact CTA and supplied logo into moving felt; unspecified
placement; user has not made a paid-ad disclosure decision.

Pass: deterministic exact graphics in post, correct perspective/occlusion if
integrated, unresolved safe zone and separate user disclosure decision.
Fail: guarantee spelling from generation, invent margins or quote invented consent.
