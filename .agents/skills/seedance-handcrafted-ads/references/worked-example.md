# Worked Example: Folded Frame

Hypothetical prompt-only exercise, not a generated or inspected ad. The selected
references and approved choices are assumptions for this example only.

## Creative contrast before pose counts

The two-panel reveal below is a construction study, not the default selling
idea. For a fictional travel notebook with selected closed/open views, propose
a paper journey: three connected accordion folds rise from a crafted set around
the unchanged notebook, forming a path that returns toward its cover. Paper
edge, fold and support continuity make the journey tangible. This is a metaphor,
not proof of travel durability, handmade manufacture or sustainable materials.

Quiet version: one fold opens, pauses at a turn, then reveals the path's return
to the notebook. Expressive version: three folds rise in a staggered cascade,
changing the set from flat to navigable before settling around the hero.
Choose one treatment within the source count, not both as automatic generations.
The product never becomes paper and exact type remains in post.

**Weak/repair test:** More paper texture on two panels does not give their
opening a purpose. Make construction reveal a relationship. Do not substitute
random melting, texture boil or camera jitter for material invention.

## Brief and Authority

A nine-second square ad for a pale-green cylindrical tin with a fitted lid.
The proposition is to reveal its round silhouette by folding away a two-panel
paper screen. The tin is the canonical hero in a paper set, not a paper replica.
No claim about its contents, recyclability, manufacture or performance is supplied.

- Image 1: selected front three-quarter acquired product photograph.
- Image 2: selected slightly elevated view of the same closed tin.
- Canonical descriptor: `pale-green cylindrical tin with a fitted flat lid`.
- Both images establish a plain finish for this fictional product. No rear
  printing or underside is established; these remain unknown.
- The brief explicitly requests a simulated paper stop-motion look, exactly
  one continuous source, no cuts or retries, with post sound and CTA.
- Exact supplied CTA: `Discover the range`. No logo or destination URL is supplied.
- Delivery is 24 FPS. Approximately twelve pose changes per second is the
  intended aesthetic, not a requested model FPS or guaranteed exposure pattern.

## Construction Ledger

The tin stands upright on a neutral tabletop and never moves or opens.
Two cream paper panels occupy a plane in front of it with a gap through which
the tin is already partly recognizable. Their lower edges have visible scored
folds attached to the tabletop. Each panel bends at its own bottom fold and
lowers toward the camera, exposing more of the tin without touching it.

| Element | Fixed Facts | Permitted Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tin | One closed tin, same lid, colour and proportions, base supported | None |
| Left panel | One flat cream rectangle, bottom fold at frame-left foreground | Fold toward camera from upright to flat |
| Right panel | One flat cream rectangle, bottom fold at frame-right foreground | Same action after the left panel settles |
| Table and wall | Neutral set, fixed scale and position | None |

Paper edge texture and scored folds are the two craft cues. No exposure flicker,
camera jitter, changing tin texture or floating pieces are requested.

## Shot and State Contract

| Phase / Source | Approximate Window | Event and End State | Cadence |

4-6 seconds: the right panel lowers in the same way until flat. Both panels

6-9 seconds: hold both panels flat and the full tin silhouette unobstructed.

## Request and Post Handoff

Request intent: one nine-second 1:1 source at the requested supported target,
continuous take, `generate_audio: false` where supported. Caller verification
resolves duration and current parameter support; delivery FPS belongs to
finishing, not an invented generation parameter.

Preserve the two separate folding actions and the final hold. At 24 FPS, exact
two-frame holds would mean twelve pose updates per second, but the prompt
cannot guarantee them. The brief asks for approximate stepping, so retain a
successful source's pose character. If exact cadence is later requested, post
must inspect/select/repeat appropriate frames; missing bend poses cannot be
manufactured by simple duplication. Do not interpolate away the stepped intent.

Add two stylized paper-fold cues at observed movement, not assumed timestamps.
They are not actual recordings of this setup. Add `Discover the range` exactly
as deterministic text on the closing hold. Placement is unspecified, so safe-zone
layout remains provisional. No generated paper letters or logo are permitted.
An actual branded tin would still need printed-mark inspection against its
acquired reference.

## Acceptance and Policy

Check two panels throughout, visible hinge causality, no product material
change, no unknown view, stable scale, intended stepping and clear recognition.
Playback is needed to distinguish stepping from jitter. No output check has run.
