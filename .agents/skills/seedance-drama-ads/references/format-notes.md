# Drama Ad Format Notes

## Source and limits

Source: public Drama Ads introduction, page text retrieved 2026-11-16.
The source URL and attribution are recorded in this skill's `sourceNotes`
in [skills-lock.json](../../../../skills-lock.json).

The FAQ describes a drama ad as a short vertical product story with "a hook,
a twist and a reveal in under a minute" for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
It accepts a product image, reference or website as a starting input. The
page describes choosing an idea, characters and visual style, reviewing logo,
colors, tone and selling points, requesting ads per product in a batch, and
revising results through chat while retaining brand identity.

Its format menu includes short drama, singing ads, talking objects, 3D cartoon,
crochet and felt, and cinematic ads. This mixes narrative formats, sound
formats and visual treatments; do not treat every item as a drama requirement.

This is evidence of the vendor's advertised workflow, not measured product
performance, verified output quality, platform specifications or Seedance
capabilities. No example video was inspected for this update. Do not infer
shot counts, exact cut times, dialogue, acting quality or motion recipes from
the page text. Do not claim these formats outperform other ads or are
currently trending without separate evidence.
