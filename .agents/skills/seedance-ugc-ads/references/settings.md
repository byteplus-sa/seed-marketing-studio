# Setting library

Read one entry when a brief asks for a setting or leaves the location open.
Each setting is a **location sentence plus a light sentence** to drop into the
environment part of a mode prompt. Settings are creative starting points, not
performance evidence.

## Rules for every setting

- **A setting changes where, not what.** It never changes product facts, the
  hook, locked copy or the CTA.
- **The brief supplies the market.** Entries name no country, city, culture or
  language; architecture, signage language, wardrobe and props come from the
  brief, and an agent never assumes one.
- **Keep generated surfaces text-free.** Describe recurring non-product props
  positively as plain ("plain white ceramic mug, nothing printed"); signs,
  screens and packaging other than the product invent lettering.
- **Heightened settings are conceptual staging.** A surreal environment must
  not imply a product capability the facts do not support.
- **Name the light.** Keep the named source in the light sentence so polished
  modes can carry it into their shot plan.

## Realistic settings

### Kitchen Counter <!-- id: setting-kitchen-counter -->
- **Location:** A tidy home kitchen counter with a plain stone surface, a
  closed cabinet run behind and nothing printed in view.
- **Light:** Soft daylight from a window frame-left, with a warm under-cabinet
  practical as a gentle fill.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-how-to, ugc-unboxing, product-review, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Food packaging and appliances grow labels; keep them out or plain.

### Bathroom Vanity <!-- id: setting-bathroom-vanity -->
- **Location:** A clean bathroom vanity with a wide mirror, a plain white basin
  and one folded plain towel.
- **Light:** Even light from a frosted window and a mirror-top practical,
  keeping the face and product free of hard shadows.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-how-to, product-review, ugc-unboxing
- **Risks:** Mirror reflections double the product or flip hands; state which
  side the reflection is on or angle the camera away from the mirror.

### Bedroom Window <!-- id: setting-bedroom-window -->
- **Location:** A bright bedroom corner with a made bed, a full-length mirror
  and a clear patch of floor for movement.
- **Light:** Diffused morning daylight through sheer curtains from frame-right.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-virtual-try-on, ugc-unboxing, product-review
- **Risks:** Hems lost at the frame edge; leave floor space and full-body framing.

### Desk Workspace <!-- id: setting-desk-workspace -->
- **Location:** A home desk with a closed laptop, one plant and a plain notebook,
  with a bare wall behind.
- **Light:** Cool daylight from a side window plus a warm desk-lamp practical.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-how-to, product-review, product-showcase, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Screens and notebooks invent text; keep the laptop closed and pages blank.

### Parked Car <!-- id: setting-parked-car -->
- **Location:** The front seat of a parked car, engine off, phone-shot from the
  dashboard toward the driver's seat.
- **Light:** Overcast daylight through the windscreen, flat and even.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, product-review, ugc-unboxing
- **Risks:** Implying use while driving; keep the car visibly parked. Dashboard
  displays invent text; keep them off.

### City Sidewalk <!-- id: setting-city-sidewalk -->
- **Location:** A quiet sidewalk beside a plain building wall, with soft
  out-of-focus passers-by far in the background.
- **Light:** Late-afternoon sun from behind the presenter as a rim, with bounce
  from the pavement on the face.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-virtual-try-on, product-review, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Shop signs and posters invent lettering; describe plain walls and
  keep background people unidentifiable.

### Park Path <!-- id: setting-park-path -->
- **Location:** A paved path through an open park lawn with trees in soft
  focus and a single plain bench.
- **Light:** Bright open shade under the trees, with dappled sun on the path.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, ugc-how-to, ugc-virtual-try-on, product-showcase, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Dappled light flickers across labels; place the product in open shade.

### Cafe Table <!-- id: setting-cafe-table -->
- **Location:** A small round cafe table by a window, with a plain ceramic cup
  and saucer and no menus or signage in view.
- **Light:** Window daylight frame-left with warm pendant practicals behind.
- **Valid modes:** ugc, product-review, ugc-unboxing, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Cups, menus and window decals grow text; keep them plain or out of frame.

## Heightened and surreal settings

### Colour Sweep Studio <!-- id: setting-colour-sweep -->
- **Location:** An infinite seamless colour sweep in one brand-supplied colour,
  with the product on a matching plinth.
- **Light:** A large soft key from above frame-left and a white rim from behind.
- **Valid modes:** product-showcase, tv-spot, wild-card, virtual-try-on
- **Risks:** The sweep colour tints the product; check product colour against
  its reference. Ask for the colour rather than inventing a brand palette.

### Oversized Product World <!-- id: setting-oversized-product -->
- **Location:** A presenter standing beside a building-sized replica of the
  product in an open plaza, the replica's proportions exact.
- **Light:** Clear midday sun casting a long crisp shadow from the replica.
- **Valid modes:** wild-card, tv-spot, product-showcase
- **Risks:** Scale play distorts proportions or label layout; bind the product
  reference and keep the label side facing camera.

### Zero-Gravity Room <!-- id: setting-zero-gravity -->
- **Location:** A white room where the product and a few plain objects float
  slowly at different heights.
- **Light:** Soft even top light from a ceiling panel, no hard shadows.
- **Valid modes:** wild-card, product-showcase, tv-spot
- **Risks:** Floating objects multiply or merge; count every object and name
  its position. Liquids must not appear to spill unless supported.

### Cloud Platform <!-- id: setting-cloud-platform -->
- **Location:** A smooth plain plinth on a sea of clouds under open sky.
- **Light:** Low golden sun from frame-right, with soft blue sky fill.
- **Valid modes:** product-showcase, tv-spot, wild-card, virtual-try-on
- **Risks:** Implied lightness, comfort or "heavenly" claims; keep it as mood,
  not a property of the product.

### Mirror Water Floor <!-- id: setting-mirror-water -->
- **Location:** A shallow, perfectly still water floor stretching to the
  horizon, reflecting the product and the sky.
- **Light:** Dusk sky glow from behind with a soft frontal key on the product.
- **Valid modes:** product-showcase, tv-spot, wild-card, virtual-try-on
- **Risks:** Reflections duplicate the product or mirror its label; electronics
  standing in water imply waterproofing; use a plinth unless water resistance is supported.

### Monochrome Set <!-- id: setting-monochrome-set -->
- **Location:** A room where walls, floor and every plain prop share one
  supplied colour, the product the only differently coloured object.
- **Light:** Broad soft light from a large window frame-left, low contrast.
- **Valid modes:** product-showcase, tv-spot, wild-card, virtual-try-on, ugc-virtual-try-on
- **Risks:** Garment or product colour shifts toward the set colour; check
  against the reference.
