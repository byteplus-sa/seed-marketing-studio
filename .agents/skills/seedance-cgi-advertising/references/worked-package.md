# Worked CGI Package

This is a hypothetical example, not a generated or validated result. Assume a
fictional compact wireless speaker has a selected product photograph showing
a graphite cylinder, woven grille and flat circular top. No performance claim,
brand copy or extra view is supplied.

## Treatment

The speaker anchors a folded-paper landscape whose rising terraces reveal
its silhouette, ending on a stable product view. This visual metaphor suggests
rhythm, not measured loudness or sound coverage.

Request intent, separate from the prompt: a ten-second picture-only clip in the
brief's chosen ratio and supported resolution. The final ad also contains an
independently timed deterministic product ending. The caller verifies current
duration and audio support; these choices are illustrative, not defaults.

## References

`@Image 1` is the selected product photograph, used only for visible product
identity. The scenery is described in text. There is no storyboard binding,
invented second reference or claim that the photograph reveals the back panel.

## Shot plan

| Window | Event and purpose | Camera and light | Exit / sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 s | A folded sheet rises behind the upright speaker; establish product | Medium low three-quarter view; normal-lens perspective; slow lateral move right; broad off-frame source camera-left | Rising fold crosses behind hero; dry paper movement |
| 3-7 s | Terraces unfold outward; reveal scale | Wide high angle; moderately wide lens with deep focus; smooth pullback; same left source plus soft off-frame rear edge light | Foreground plane covers lens left to right; swelling pulse |
| 7-10 s | Plane clears to reveal speaker centered on a flat platform | Medium frontal view; normal-lens perspective; short push ending at rest; soft key from camera-left | Stable silhouette and clear space; one restrained landing accent |

Windows are approximate generation direction. The editor measures the returned
actions before placing sound accents or committing cut points.
Plan a generated hard cut at the first boundary and a roughly one-second
generated wipe spanning the second. Keep the speaker as focal target until
occluded, then return focus to its grille as the plane clears.

## Complete picture prompt

```text
@Image 1 defines the speaker's visible silhouette, graphite color, woven grille
and flat circular top. Use those product attributes throughout; the paper
landscape is a separate environment.

One upright speaker anchors a sculptural paper landscape. The speaker stays
rigid while broad ivory sheets unfold into terraces around its platform.
The world has crisp paper edges, soft surface fibers and believable contact
shadows. Broad light comes from an off-frame area source at camera-left,
with a subtle off-frame rear source separating the graphite silhouette.
Added graphic text and closing artwork belong to the separate edit.

0-3s: Medium low three-quarter view with natural normal-lens perspective.
One broad sheet rises behind the speaker
into a shallow arch. Track slowly right from the speaker's front-left corner
to its front, keeping its top and grille visible. End with the speaker framed
inside the arch.

3-7s: Cut to a wide high-angle view of that same speaker on its platform,
using a moderately wide lens with deep focus.
The surrounding sheets unfold outward into three broad terraces. Pull back
smoothly to reveal their scale. A foreground plane rises and travels left to
right until it completely fills the view.

7-10s: Complete the roughly one-second wipe as the ivory plane clears left to
right, revealing the same speaker centered upright on a single flat platform.
Medium frontal framing with normal-lens perspective, with a
short slow push toward the woven grille that settles before the end.
The broad left highlight remains soft and the product silhouette stays clear.
End with the speaker stable and open space around it.
```

No Audio slot is needed for this picture-only request. The caller records the
supported audio-off parameter separately. Do not claim the clip itself contains
the final score.

## Edit and sound handoff

- Measure the actual fold and wipe moments. Align a dry paper effect with the
  fold, a filtered sweep with the occlusion, and one low accent with the reveal.
- Compose or source a suitably authorized restrained instrumental pulse.
  Timbral direction is a brief, not a claim that a soundtrack already exists.
- Use the selected photograph for the exact product-ending graphic. Preserve
  its original dimensions and proportions; do not invent unseen product views.
- Record which generated spans enter the final edit. Preserve original prompt
  snapshots even if the cut excludes a shot.
- Check runtime after overlaps: `final duration = sum(used segment durations)
  - sum(overlaps)`. Include the product ending once. Retiming and padding must
  be explicit decisions, not hidden attempts to reach a nominal duration.

## Acceptance criteria

The product is recognizable in the opening; only one speaker exists; grille,
top and silhouette remain consistent; the plane covers the entire frame before
the scene changes; the final product hold is stable; sound accents follow
actual visible events. A correct last card does not excuse a warped earlier
speaker. Do not claim temporal or sonic passes from still frames.
