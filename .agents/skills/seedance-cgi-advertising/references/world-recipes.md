# CGI World Recipes

These are hypothetical creative starting points, not tested model presets.
Select one governing material behavior and adapt it to the supplied product.
Do not infer ingredients, benefits or a market from a recipe.

| World | Primary event | Camera motivation | Transition pair | Sonic direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soft architecture | Rounded foam forms rise around a rigid hero object | Pull from surface detail to reveal monumental scale | Arch fills frame → matching arch frames product | Rounded pulse, airy release |
| Optical chamber | A transparent lens travels past the product | Track its rim while keeping product shape readable | Lens covers view → clear chamber opens | Glassy accent, brief filtered sweep |
| Folded landscape | A sheet unfolds into terraces beneath the product | Low glide exposes each rising plane | Fold edge wipes frame → next plane continues vector | Dry fold, restrained bass landing |
| Ribbon sculpture | A ribbon circles the hero without wrapping its label | Partial orbit reveals path and product in alternating focus | Ribbon covers view → same-color sweep clears | Fabric swish, accelerating pulse |
| Ingredient constellation | Supplied serving ingredients orbit a food pack, then settle beside a dish | Follow one ingredient into a wider serving scene | Circular motion → bowl rim match cut | Light percussion, cooking ambience |
| Kinetic precision | Fins or tiles align around a device | Track alignment toward an undistorted hero view | Moving panel occlusion → clean frontal reveal | Mechanical ticks, single low impact |
| Floating terrain | A platform lifts with a product fixed upright | Rise beside platform to expose a surreal horizon | Horizon line match → product silhouette payoff | Spacious swell, sparse accents |
| Outdoor scale illusion | Oversized product stands in a selected believable environment | Parallax reveals scale through stable foreground geometry | Foreground object wipe → another consistent viewpoint | Location ambience, controlled reveal accent |

Outdoor concepts must not imply an installation really occurred. Obtain
appropriate location/reference authority through the caller. Avoid incidental
brand signage and do not make bystanders into endorsements.

## Camera choices

- **Macro to monumental:** begin on an identifiable detail and pull back to
  reveal scale. Preserve texture scale until the deliberate reveal.
- **Orbit to resolution:** use a partial orbit that ends on the label side,
  then settle long enough to recognize the product. Constant spinning hides it.
- **Tunnel traversal:** give entry, internal landmarks and a distinct exit.
  Empty forward flight is not an escalation.
- **Tracking an object:** follow a designated droplet, tile or petal toward
  the hero. State which object remains the tracking target.
- **Overhead descent:** descend from a readable arrangement to a specific
  detail. Keep product count and relative positions stable.
- **Held hero:** use stillness for legibility after the energetic beat. Record
  the reason; the material or environment may continue moving gently.

## Transition decisions

| Mechanism | Required outgoing state | Required incoming state | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material wipe | A single sheet fully covers frame along a named direction | Matching sheet clears in the same direction | Partial coverage exposes a jump |
| Shape match | Shape at stated position, scale and orientation | Corresponding shape with compatible motion | Two centered circles have incompatible perspective |
| Portal | Camera crosses a defined opening with an occlusion window | New world becomes visible beyond the opening | Ring approaches without traversing |
| Foreground pass | Foreground geometry hides the lens | Same geometry or compatible darkness releases it | Product visibly morphs during overlap |
| Light sweep | Highlight crosses a defined surface | Highlight reveals the next surface in corresponding position | Unmotivated flash washes out the label |
| Clean cut | Outgoing action reaches a readable endpoint | Incoming composition provides contrast | Adding a dissolve weakens impact |

Choose implementation per join, not once for the whole film. An edit may need
a short hard cut even when generated scenes contain flowing internal changes.
Keep double-image crossfades away from exact-label holds.

## Custom world

1. Identify a visible product design cue or supported fact.
2. Convert it into a material or geometric rule.
3. Choose one physically observable event that expresses the rule.
4. Make the camera reveal information about that event.
5. Choose a transition with matching endpoints, not just a name.
6. Return to a stable product state.
7. Assign one sonic signature; use contrasts within it rather than piling on
   every impact, whoosh and bass effect.

Reject a custom idea when it depends on an unsupported performance claim.
Choose an aesthetic metaphor instead of disguising that claim as CGI.
