# Repairs and Review

These are reusable diagnoses informed by production experience, not universal
model defects or promises that the suggested repair will work. Keep evidence
for a specific failure in its campaign, with the actual artifact and span.

| Observed symptom | Inspect before deciding | Scoped repair and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging warps during a hero closeup | Compare silhouette, pump/cap and label against the selected image at actual display size | Remove the failing span, use a truthful exact-product layer, or request a targeted take within the requested scope; an exact end card alone is insufficient |
| Source bottle is cropped | Check original image boundaries, not a generated reconstruction | Frame the supplied crop honestly or obtain a complete approved view; do not call invented unseen geometry exact |
| Light stands or emitters enter the world | Inspect whether the prompt names a studio without emitter placement | Describe the light's result and put the physical source off-frame; retain visible fixtures only if the set treatment asks for them |
| Every product gets the same ring tunnel | Compare event, scale, camera and sound across the collection | Change the governing world rule and payoff, not only colors |
| Portal never leads anywhere | Watch camera travel through the opening and the revealed endpoint | Add a traversed boundary and incoming state, or make a clean edit between compatible views |
| Crossfade doubles the label | View the overlap at playback speed and frame by frame | Move overlap to an occlusion or abstract insert, shorten it, or use a hard cut |
| Removing a bad take makes the film slow | Measure retained coverage and inspect the intended energy | Re-plan runtime, add usable coverage within scope or shorten holds; disclose retiming instead of pretending the original kinetic plan survived |
| Broad soundtrack misses impacts | Listen against the actual cut, including any retimed spans | Keep score as bed and place separate event-level SFX; do not claim frame synchronization from a prose prompt |
| Procedural fallback feels repetitive | Listen for repetitive melody, density and identical transition accents | Change phrase arrangement, introduce rests and vary accents; report synthetic timbre instead of claiming realism |
| Reviewers disagree about clicks or silence | Re-listen to the exact reported boundary and inspect stream timing | Record conflicting reports and focused findings; unresolved evidence is not a fabricated pass |

## Minimal final sanity

The caller checks the actual selected edit, not a superseded take:

1. **Identity:** selected reference and used product remain consistent through
   movement. Inspect all product-bearing spans, not just the closing card.
2. **Motion:** each primary event happens, camera travel is intentional, and
   geometry/counts do not jump accidentally.
3. **Transitions:** inspect both sides and overlaps; framing, direction and
   focal target support the intended relationship.
4. **Sound:** listen to the complete mix and its joins, event accents and tail.
   Waveforms, loudness measurements and speech recognition do not replace this.
5. **Ending:** exact supplied copy/product image is legible, correctly cropped
   and proportioned; safe-zone checks use the actual placement specification.
6. **Technical:** measure dimensions, duration, audio/video alignment, frame
   rate, decode, target loudness and true peak against the brief. Do not reuse a
   fixed campaign target as a universal delivery standard.
7. **Lineage:** source spans, retiming, generated scenes, exact graphics,
   soundtrack and any reused music are identified correctly.

Use `pass`, `fail` or `needs-human` only with actual evidence. Separate the
agent's creative decision from brand approval, rights and paid-ad disclosure.
Generation success alone does not approve a take.

## Revision record

Return a concise delta when revising a prompt:

```text
Preserved: product reference, palette, opening action, selected audio route.
Failure: the product silhouette stretches during the final orbit.
Change: end the orbit sooner and land on the existing exact-product ending.
Acceptance: no stretched silhouette in the used span; ending retains intended energy.
Tradeoff: less generated closeup time; no claim that discarded frames were repaired.
```

This is a hypothetical editorial option, not authorization to modify locked
footage. Respect the actual requested delta and production approval mode.

## Production handoff cautions

Do not turn a prior workaround into a required workflow. A caller may use a
different soundtrack route, shorter film, native audio or a continuous take.
Runtime and audio constraints follow the actual request.

When publishing is requested, show exact prompts only beside the results they
produced or contributed to. Distinguish a finished multi-source edit from one
model output; omitted scenes and rejected soundtracks are not delivered results.
Keep local file paths, provider payloads, signed URLs and reviewer tooling out
of reader-facing prose. Successful uploads or Preview markup do not establish
in-client playback. This skill provides lineage notes, not publishing commands.
