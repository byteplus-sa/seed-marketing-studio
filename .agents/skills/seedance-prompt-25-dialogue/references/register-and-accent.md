# Register and Accent

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md).

## Register

- Record formality per speaker pair: boss and employee, elder and youth,
  spouses, strangers. Do not flatten them into one voice.
- Preserve honorifics, family terms and politeness particles exactly as
  approved; they carry status in many markets.
- Match period and region. Literary or dated language is valid when the
  character calls for it.
- For new drafts, choose line length from the intended pace and available
  duration. Do not shorten approved lines just to meet a fixed line count.

## Code-switching

- Name the dominant language and the switching pattern in the dialogue-language
  line, for example "English-dominant with short phrases in <language>".
- Preserve the approved switching pattern, word positions and delivery.
- If a current listening review identifies a problem at a switch, isolate the
  affected word and delivery. Moving it or changing volume is an option only
  within authorized revision scope, not a universal remedy.

## Accent

- Distinguish language from accent. "English with an <region> accent" is a
  request, not a guarantee; verify by listening.
- Name one accent per character and keep it across scenes. Reuse the same
  wording in every prompt for that character.
- Do not use accent direction to caricature a group.

## Subtitles

- Never bake subtitles into generated video. Add them in post
  (FFmpeg or HyperFrames) from the approved script.
- Provide a translation for every line not in the subtitle language.
- The subtitle text follows the locked words, not the heard words.
