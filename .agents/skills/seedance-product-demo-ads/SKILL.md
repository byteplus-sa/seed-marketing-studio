# Seedance Product Demo Ads

Make a supported operation understandable through visible cause and effect.
Remain an independent prompt-only leaf. Never acquire assets, generate media,
render, publish or change production state. The caller owns execution.

## Scope and inputs

Choose a mechanism demonstration, a use procedure or a problem-solution ad.
The problem must lead to a visible action, not merely a complaint followed by
a beauty shot. Service demonstrations can show documented activities; they
must not invent a physical product or a guaranteed customer outcome.
Exact software screens, cursor paths and UI explanations belong to a
deterministic explainer workflow, even when marketed as a "product demo."

Read supplied material and collect only what the requested scope needs:

- Product identity, selected views, operation instructions and limitations.
- One audience question, supported benefit and intended next action.
- Claim sources with exact wording, conditions, date and target-market scope.
- Problem context and whether any comparison or result has real evidence.
- Runtime, ratio, placement, reveal deadline and locked copy.
- Requested generation count, continuous-take or native-cut constraint.
- Setting, permitted contact/actions, language, audio and exact-graphics needs.
- Existing decisions, review_mode, approval_mode and permitted revision span.

Ask about consequential gaps. Without instructions, do not invent how a
mechanism works. Offer a claim-free exterior treatment or a provisional
state table, explicitly unfinished, while asking for the missing operation.
Do not fabricate filenames, selected references, destination URLs or prices.
No market, language, cast, camera style or duration is built in.

Read [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md) for the hypothetical
worked package and when testing or revising this skill. Its facts and timing
are illustrative inputs, never defaults or actual product evidence.

## 1. Choose the demonstrated proposition

Before the state ledger, connect the hook, viewer motivation, development,
payoff and product role. Start with a recognizable question or constraint,
then make the supported answer satisfying to see. A mechanism study may be
quiet, but a full ad needs a reason to watch that mechanism. Do not add an
orbit or a beauty hold to disguise a demonstration with no meaningful question.
Keep this reasoning outside prompt prose; carry only its visible action and
essential operation, safety and continuity constraints into the prompt.

Write `viewer question -> supported operation -> visible end state`.
Prefer one operation that can be inspected to several asserted benefits.
For a problem-solution structure, establish the actual constraint before the
product acts; end on the supported state that addresses that constraint.
Do not exaggerate ordinary inconvenience into danger or blame the user.

| Structure | Reusable progression | Necessary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism reveal | Resting state, contact, actuation, resulting state | Supplied operation and relevant views |
| Use procedure | Setup, ordered actions, completion check | Instructions, prerequisites and warnings |
| Problem-solution | Visible constraint, intervention, supported change | Both the problem context and claimed change |
| Comparison | Matched starting conditions, each action, qualified result | Comparator, method, conditions and authentic evidence |

Treat apparent speed, effortless force, capacity, protection, cleaning and
before/after imagery as claims, even without spoken words. A generated
demonstration illustrates known operation; it is not a test or proof.
Do not turn a time-compressed edit into a speed claim.

Maintain `claim_id / source / exact scope / conditions / usable wording /
status / affected shots`. Missing substantiation excludes the claim from
both prompt and post copy. Preserve qualifications and applicable warnings.
Time-sensitive facts need freshness confirmation before delivery.

## 2. Build an inspectable state chain

List the initial arrangement before writing camera prose. Name each counted
part, position, orientation and functional state. For moving parts, record
what supports them, what force moves them and where the movement stops.
Separate the operator's action from the product's response.

```text
demo_id / viewer question / claim_ids / supplied operating source
preconditions / item count / setup and safety limits
state_before / operator action / contact point / visible mechanism
state_after / observable completion cue / unsupported inference to avoid
continuity anchors / necessary view / occlusion window
```

An action must not hide its own evidence. Keep the relevant latch, hinge,
connector or material in view through contact and response. If a hand covers
the key event, change angle or propose an insert within the allowed cut plan.
Never use an unseen jump from closed to open as a mechanism demonstration.
Keep hand anatomy and frame side explicit where handling is ambiguous.

For before/after requests, separate authentic supplied evidence from generated
illustration. Record capture conditions and authorized usage for real evidence.
Do not synthesize "before" dirt, damage or defects to make an unproved outcome
look measured. A disclaimer does not substantiate an unsupported result.

## 3. Map states to shots and sources

```text
shot_id / source_id / approximate window / demonstration job
size / angle / lens intent / camera start, path and end
named light source / key side / quality / static reason if applicable
state_in / operator action / visible product response / state_out
count and positions / contact and occlusion / identity anchors
reference roles / claim_ids / dialogue or sound / transition and handles
```

Choose views for comprehension: context establishes setup, detail reveals the
mechanism, a wider finish confirms the resulting arrangement. These can be
phases of one take; they are not a mandatory three-cut formula.
Use stable framing when it exposes small movement; explain the reason.
Avoid a camera orbit that hides the action merely to add energy.

Keep editorial shots separate from provider source generations. Preserve the
requested generation count and any continuous take. One generation may have
native cuts when permitted; a continuous take cannot quietly become a montage.
Select source durations only from capabilities verified at runtime by the
caller. Prompt timing is approximate. Precise cuts, retiming, split screens
and frame-exact synchronization belong to post, with suitable source handles.
If action density or locked speech does not fit, surface the conflict and
propose a smaller demonstration or a duration change, never extra generations.

## 4. Bind identity and rights

Bind only supplied selected references, with ordered roles for product,
operator and environment. State silhouette, component count, finish, colors
and visible markings. Request unseen views or constrain the camera rather
than promising exact geometry from a single photograph.
Planning boards are not identity references.

Respect authorization for product imagery, footage, human likeness, voice and
music. A supplied photo or public review is not blanket permission to reuse.
Synthetic operators may demonstrate actions but cannot claim real purchases,
test results or personal histories. No fabricated "verified buyer" framing.
Provider privacy rejection is not permission to disguise a person's identity.

Keep added overlays, CTA, logos, captions, prices and UI exact in deterministic
post. Inspect printed product labels against the reference throughout handling;
reference conditioning does not guarantee fidelity. Where exact appearance
is essential, propose authorized real imagery or compositing with valid views.
Leave space based on the actual placement template; unknown safe zones remain
unverified, not invented universal margins.

## 5. Write the complete prompt and edit handoff

Return a complete prompt per requested source, not just a list of shot names:

```text
Subject: selected bindings, identity anchors, operator and initial setup.
Action: ordered states, contact, response, completion and approximate timing.
Environment: support surfaces, space, background and incidental plain props.
Visual style: requested realism or declared illustration, materials and light.
Camera: each view or continuous path, lens intent, readable contact, end frame.
Audio: exact authorized speech, language, event sounds and soundtrack ownership.
```

Carry all necessary state and camera facts into the prompt itself. Keep
unresolved bindings outside it and mark an incomplete package provisional.
State no added text or graphic logos in generated footage without erasing
authorized physical markings already carried by the product reference.

Separate request intent from prose: selected model, requested ratio,
runtime, generation count, native cuts, reference order and audio route.
The caller verifies supported fields; do not invent API parameters.
For silent or post-audio work specify `generate_audio: false` when supported;
an authorized route returning audio needs explicit mute/discard before mixing.
For native speech preserve the exact words and allow onset, pauses and tail;
measure actual delivery rather than imposing a universal words-per-second rate.

The edit handoff lists source ranges, state-matched cut points, handles,
permitted retiming, audio owner, exact post copy and its claim IDs, CTA
destination, placement-template source and open fidelity issues.
Never splice away a safety step or conceal a failed operation.

## 6. Revise and verify narrowly

For a bounded revision identify the changed state, dependent shot, claim and
edit boundary. Preserve all unaffected locks, generation count and sound.
If only a latch view is unreadable, propose a changed angle for that span;
do not redesign the entire ad or silently substitute an exact-photo solution.

Check that the operation can be followed, contact precedes response, parts
retain count and geometry, and the final state matches the supported claim.
Check product recognition by the requested reveal point and label stability
throughout. Review motion by playback and sound by listening; still frames
cannot establish either. Report pass, fail or untested with evidence locators.
Prompt-only checks cannot certify physical performance or platform approval.

Honor review_mode independently of approval_mode. Prompt-only work generates
nothing; downstream production and review remain caller-owned.
Every new Seedance 2.5 generation requires `resolution: "480p"` and boolean
`draft: true` in both review modes. Promote only selected native Drafts.
Surface source-count conflicts before generation; never bypass drafts or
silently expand scope.
Rights, claims and the user's paid-delivery disclosure decision remain separate
from creative approval. Do not invent disclosure text or a user's decision.

Return proposition, evidence gaps, state/shot/source records, complete prompts,
request intent, edit/audio/graphics handoff and targeted QA. For a narrow request
return just the changed material and its dependencies.
Optional caller composition may use film-production for execution,
seedance-prompt-25 for model grammar, and exact-graphics or shot specialists.
These are prose hints, not dependencies or commands to other skills.
