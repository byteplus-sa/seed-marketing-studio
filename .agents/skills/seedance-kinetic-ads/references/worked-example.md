# Worked Example: One Subject, Three Treatments

This is a hypothetical prompt-only exercise, not a generated or reviewed ad.
All references below are assumed supplied for the exercise. In real work,
replace those assumptions with actual selected files before binding them.

## Brief and reference authority

A twelve-second 9:16 ad for a plain square folding desk lamp. The audience
should notice its compact geometry and opening action. No brightness, battery
life or portability claims are supplied. A generic hands-only demonstration
is permitted; no real-person likeness, speech or added copy is requested.
A separately mixed percussive score and contact sounds are requested.

- Image 1: selected three-quarter photo of the folded lamp.
- Image 2: selected three-quarter photo of the open lamp.
- Image 3: selected hinge detail.
- The brief confirms that the arm pivots from folded to open without changing
  component count. The photographs establish a matte blue square base, one
  straight arm and one square light head. No unseen rear view is established.

Proposition: repeated square geometry links detail, opening action and the
whole lamp; a fast approach resolves into a calm, readable silhouette.

## Cinematic sequence

All windows below are provisional editorial targets, not promised model cuts.
The final cut is twelve seconds; generated source durations need not sum to
twelve seconds. Select supported source durations after the caller resolves
capabilities, with trimming room around each action.

| Shot | Edit Window | Event and Coverage | Continuity / Edit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 0.0-1.2 s | Low three-quarter close shot; camera slides right beside the stationary folded lamp, revealing its square outline | Establish recognizable whole product; keep base planted |
| B | 1.2-1.8 s | Overhead hinge macro; short forward push across the seam | Hard cut from wide silhouette to detail; extracted from its own longer source |
| C | 1.8-5.0 s | High three-quarter view; a hand opens the lamp as a small camera arc reveals depth | Physical pivot, not a telescoping arm; support remains fixed |
| D | 5.0-5.7 s | Tight oblique view of the head's front edge; short leftward slide | Brief shape insert, not an unseen rear view |
| E | 5.7-9.3 s | Lower medium shot of the open lamp; rapid approach decelerates before the head fills the frame | Base stays visible until late approach; preserve screen orientation |
| F | 9.3-12.0 s | Held three-quarter whole-product frame | Clean cut to a readable silhouette; longer release after the burst |

Anchor the rectangular area light above the table's west edge and the white
bounce beyond its east edge, both off-frame. Cameras stay in the table's
southern half; the key reads frame-left in A/C/E/F, upper-left in overhead B
and rear-left in oblique D. The light stays fixed during camera movement.
Keep the tabletop matte and background neutral. No grade is specified.
Maintain camera clearance above the table, believable focus and consistent
surface texture.

Generate A, B, C, D and E as independently controllable actions. F may use a
stable matching portion of E only if the actual footage supplies the framing;
otherwise obtain a separate held view. Do not assume an unused angle exists.
For a full deliverable, each source receives its own complete prompt.

## Complete Source Prompt for C

```text
References:
@Image 1 defines the folded lamp's identity, colour and proportions.
@Image 2 defines the open configuration of that same lamp.
@Image 3 defines its hinge appearance.
Use the reference backgrounds neither as locations nor as camera framing.

Subject and action:
The matte blue lamp rests on a matte tabletop. A demonstrator's hand enters
from frame-right, grips the head's side edge and gently lifts it, pivoting
the single straight arm at the shown hinge from the folded state to the open
state in Image 2. The square base remains planted. The square head stays
attached and rigid. The hand releases once the lamp is stable, then leaves
frame-right. Show the open position at rest.

Environment:
A quiet tabletop set with clear space around the lamp, the near table edge
in the lower foreground and a neutral wall well behind the product.

Visual style:
Photographed commercial realism. One broad rectangular area light fixed above
the table's west edge creates a soft frame-left highlight, with an off-frame
white bounce beyond the east edge. Both stay fixed as the camera moves in
the southern half of the set. Preserve the matte finish and contact shadow.
Use a normal-lens perspective with enough depth of field to read the hinge,
arm and head together.

Camera:
Begin above the product in a high three-quarter medium close shot on its
reference-visible side. Arc a short distance clockwise as the arm opens,
ending before the unseen rear becomes visible. Keep the base in frame
and the camera above the tabletop. Hold the open state at the end.

Audio:
Picture is intended for a separately mixed soundtrack; no dialogue is needed.
```

Request intent: 9:16, a natural supported duration long enough for the action
and edit handles, requested delivery resolution resolved by the caller, and
picture-only generation with `generate_audio: false`. The caller verifies
parameter support before submission; the audio sentence cannot enforce it.
If an explicitly authorized alternative returns audio, mute or discard that
track before adding the separate post soundtrack.

## Edit and sound handoff

- A/B: cut from folded whole at frame centre to its hinge seam near centre;
  no continuous motion match claimed. Retain a stable tail and entry handle.
- B/C: cut from the unchanged seam to the whole folded configuration before
  the hand enters from right. Retain enough pre-action handle to read the grip.
- C/D: cut only after the hand clears and the lamp is fully open, to the same
  head's edge. Both sources need stable open-state handles.
- D/E: match the head's square edge near upper centre while the outgoing
  leftward slide settles and the incoming approach begins. Select matching
  scale from handles; otherwise use a clean cut, since no transition is locked.
- E/F: cut from the near head to the whole open silhouette, deliberately
  contrasting scale. Require settled handles on both sides, with no morph.
- All five boundaries are picture edits in post. The separately mixed score
  bridges them; only the hinge action and E's deceleration receive event cues.
- Trim B and D in post; do not request 0.6-second generation durations.
- Use a low opening pulse, one quiet hinge sound during C and a dry accent at
  E's deceleration. Leave F less dense. Confirm actual mechanical sound with
  supplied evidence or label the cue stylized, not authentic recorded foley.
- Align accents after picture selection. No track has been measured here, so
  no BPM or exact beat alignment is claimed.
- No extra graphics were requested. If a logo or CTA is later supplied, lay it
  out in post with the verified placement safe zone and revisit the hold length.

## CGI adaptation

Treat every shot as constructed, photorealistic CGI, including the hand and
tabletop. Keep the product, proposition and identity anchors. A physically
plausible CGI version can preserve every cinematic action without any
impossible event.

For an explicitly surreal alternative, make D a field of square light planes
that repeat the head's geometry. C/D cuts from the open head's square edge at
upper centre to a matching plane at that position and scale. The foreground
plane travels left to right until it covers the frame. At full coverage,
cut to E behind a matching foreground plane moving rightward out of frame.
Match plane colour, travel vector and apparent speed; both sources need
full-coverage handles. The wipe is joined in post, with one soft air accent.
These planes are metaphor, not emitted output or a brightness claim.
Do not morph the lamp into a different product.

CGI is allowed to break the background's physical rules here. Product count,
hinge geometry and surface finish remain fixed. If exact transition matching
fails, a hard cut is an acceptable disclosed editorial substitution.

## Hybrid adaptation

Keep A-C and E-F photographed-looking. Mark D alone as the synthetic insert.
Carry the same blue hue, square edge orientation and light direction through
both boundaries. Use shape-matched hard cuts at C/D and D/E, keeping the
square near upper centre; do not carry the CGI version's foreground plane
into E. Return to the grounded product in E. The transition is conceptual
and does not assert that the lamp produces floating geometry.

## Acceptance criteria

Recognition by A; stable part count, natural grip and base contact through C;
no camera intersection; meaningful size/angle contrast; readable final silhouette;
no invented product claims. Synthetic exceptions occur only where declared.
All are proposed checks. No footage, soundtrack or generated result has been
inspected in this example.
