# Performance Direction

## Applicability and Ownership

For new or materially revised character-performance scenes, `film-production`
coordinates `acting-direction` before shot design, then `seedance-shot-design`
for readable coverage and `seedance-acting-console` for Seedance cue encoding.
Use `scene-craft` for scene writing under the selected framework; its
five-element engine is optional, not a universal gate. Leaf skills consume
supplied artifacts and never invoke each other or submit generation.

The same handoff applies to character-performance hooks in `ad-variant-matrix`
and new performance in `ad-localize`. A static camera, silence, a concealed
face or a talking-head format is not an acting exemption. Canonical static
images, empty plates, product-only motion, sound beds and deterministic
graphics do not need an acting plan. Incidental extras need disposition, not
invented personal arcs. Source-preserving edits inherit performance and
inspect its preservation; new acting is allowed only within `may_change`.

## Marketing Boundaries

Preserve `must_preserve`: exact copy, spoken dialogue, CTA, legal lines,
supported claims, product identity, approved demonstrations, campaign pins
(`library.pin_current`), language/register, timing, framing, medium and outcome.
Performance direction cannot invent a testimonial, product benefit or proof.
On-screen text remains deterministic post work, not generated lettering.
An acting repair does not authorize rewriting ad copy or changing the offer.
Return duration conflicts for authorized retiming or splitting, not silent
shortening, speeding up or extra generations beyond the budget.

Localization changes only inventoried elements at the selected level. L1 text
and L2 voice preserve picture performance; L3 talent and L4 source-preserving
swaps retain action, camera, cuts and timing. A newly authored performance
requires the appropriate authorized scope, normally L5 for that beat, with a
new plan in the localized project. Locked target-language wording remains
verbatim. L3 to L5 final generation and delivery still require genuine
`localize.native_review`; an acting check never replaces human sign-off.
The source campaign remains untouched except its existing `markets/` records.
Variant rows retain the one-axis boundary, hero body, copy, CTA and budget.

## Durable Handoff

Store `## Performance plan` in the existing scene file, or the variant's
existing `shot.md` when it has no separate scene file. Record source locators,
current SHA-256, evidence limits, locks, selected method, each principal's
motive/goal/obstacle/tactic, physical channel and listener task or disposition.
Use existing beat IDs to connect:

**Trigger -> what is registered -> attempt maintained/changed -> consequence.**

Preserve intentional stillness, sustained attention, non-response, ambiguity
and quiet holds; do not require an emotion change, tactic switch, reversal or
reaction close-up. Do not invent shared agreement, backstory or new knowledge.
Shot notes identify relevant beats, visibility and critical acceptance criteria.
Prompts carry the pursuit, trigger and readable response once, not a full essay.
Reserve time for listening and response as well as speech.

Body speed, amplitude, interruptions and recovery follow approved intent,
including repeated energetic actions. Actor intensity, camera energy and edit
rhythm are independent; dialogue is not an automatic kinetic exclusion.
Evaluate required words, lip-sync, contact and listener evidence at the actual
framing and speed. Slowing an action or isolating a burst with holds is an
optional repair for demonstrated failure, not a universal acceptance rule.

For new vague work, settle the compact creative premise, attention and payoff
before execution; offer alternatives when materially different ideas remain
open. Scoped prompts inherit settled intent and need no expanded workflow.
Plan sound and editorial intent before generation in existing brief/scene
notes: what is heard before seen, what the edit reveals or withholds, and how
silence or an accent supports the intended response. No final sound asset or
new runtime is required early; final cues follow the measured edit.

Bind current plan, source, shot plan and exact request hashes in review evidence
or existing upstream hash fields where supported. No request schema or provider
parameter is added. Changed interpretation, plan or cues invalidate dependent
reviews: reopen affected work and re-review without migrating historical
projects, immutable snapshots or prior approvals automatically.

## Prompt Review

For applicable performance prompts, pass `--required-rule acting.plan_bound`
through the existing review and registration interface. This applies in both
`review_mode` values, independently of `approval_mode`. Default mode checks
only applicable required rules, not blanket strict craft checklists.

- `acting.plan_bound`: trace critical beats to partner/situation-directed
  pursuit, trigger, registration, listener disposition, readable consequence
  and timing. Isolated prompt work can provide equivalent intent inline.
  Mood-only studies remain provisional.
- Missing material evidence is `incomplete`, not a presumed pass. Dropping or
  contradicting a critical beat is MAJOR and blocks submission.
- Static/non-performance work and preservation-only edits record
  `not_applicable` with a concrete reason outside the required-rule list.
  Preservation still needs its edit checks. The interface requires `pass`
  for required rules and rejects `not_applicable`.

Reviewers check applicability even if a caller omitted the rule. These are
semantic reviewer obligations, not an automatic acting classifier. The CLI
validates declared checks and evidence structure, not performance quality.
Every new Seedance 2.5 job, including edits, extensions, retries and variants,
still starts with `resolution: "480p"` and boolean `draft: true`. Selection,
promotion and fresh final review follow the existing production policy.

## Actual-Take Acceptance

Before selecting or promoting, inspect playback and listen when audio is in
scope. Cite timestamps and beat IDs for registration, pursuit, relevant
listeners, pacing, pauses and exact delivery. A transcript alone cannot
establish acting. Verify product contact, identities, prop ownership, physical
state and continuity across cuts without inventing new action.

Missing a critical response, speaking assignment or required interaction is a
hard failure. Equivalent readable performance may pass without a particular
blink, tears or cue count. No passing take means a bounded revision within
budget or a blocked stage, never selection of the least-bad output. Unavailable
playback/listening remains unresolved. Recheck the final output and actual edit
so trimming does not remove the accepted beat.

Set a small relevant set of artistic acceptance criteria from the brief before
selection. Inspect full-speed playback of takes and the final edit with sound
when in scope, recording timestamps and beat IDs in existing review evidence.
Check the intended audience attention and payoff, meaningful sustained waiting
or deliberate ambiguity, and sound/editorial rhythm as well as technical
legibility. A correct transcript, schema or clean join is not artistic evidence.
Do not reward extra motion, cuts, turns or holds by count. Unavailable perception
or a missing critical artistic beat remains unresolved; keep repairs within
locks and budget. Default evidence stays focused and concise, strict review
stays detailed, and existing human approvals remain authoritative.
